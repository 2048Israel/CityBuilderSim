world-1: the nations' world of CityBuilderSim (Civic Ledger)

world-1.bin, and its fixture world-1-fixture.bin in the game's jar, are derived from the data sets below: their
figures selected, converted and combined by the game, not as published. Their credits follow, each in the form its
publisher asks for; docs/studies/licences.md has each licence as read on 2026-10-31 (LC1). This file ships beside
the exe as data/world-ATTRIBUTION.txt.

Natural Earth 1:10m Cultural Vectors (5.1.2): Admin 0 - Countries, Admin 0 - Boundary Lines (land), Populated Places (simple), Ports
  Credit:  Made with Natural Earth. Free vector and raster map data @ naturalearthdata.com.
  Licence: Public domain (naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use)
  Used for: whose land every 15" cell is (the owner layer: each country feature to the nation that holds it, a territory to its sovereign, the rest as areas), the land boundary lines with their two sides' owners, each nation's name for the geography, label point and node (its largest port city: a populated place within 25 km of a port), and the neighbours of a nation CERDI lacks (South Sudan)
  Source:  https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector (tag v5.1.2); https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-cultural-vectors/

CERDI-seadistance (1.1)
  Credit:  Bertoli, S., Goujon, M. and Santoni, O. (2016) The CERDI-seadistance database. CERDI Working Paper 2016/7, version 1.1. https://zenodo.org/record/240493
  Licence: CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
  Used for: the freight layer: the sea distances between 195 nations' ports, the road distances from a landlocked nation's capital to its port, and the road distances between neighbouring capitals, closed into a table of the cheapest route between any two nations
  Source:  https://zenodo.org/record/240493

BACI, international trade database at the product level, HS 2022, version 202601 (CEPII)
  Credit:  Gaulier, G. and Zignago, S. (2010) BACI: International Trade Database at the Product-Level. The 1994-2007
           Version. CEPII Working Paper 2010-23. Data: CEPII, BACI HS22 V202601 (last updated January 2026).
  Licence: Etalab Open Licence 2.0 (https://www.etalab.gouv.fr/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/open-licence.pdf)
  Used for: the trade layer: the base year's (2024) flows between the nations, by pair and good, through the
           concordance from HS 2022 to the game's goods (concordance.tsv), and each good's unit value
  Source:  https://www.cepii.fr/DATA_DOWNLOAD/baci/doc/baci_webpage.html

world-2025: the Rankings' measures file (LB1, 2026-10-30)

world-2025.json (src/main/resources/world/) is derived from the data sets below, and from world-1.bin's 2025 figures
for the few nations the World Bank does not cover (the credits above). Its meta names each source with its licence,
and the Rankings' (i) shows it; this file, beside the exe, carries the credits in full. LC1 (2026-10-31) left out
every figure whose maker's terms do not allow a sold game to ship it (docs/studies/licences.md).
