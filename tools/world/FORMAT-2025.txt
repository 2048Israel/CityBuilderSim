world-2025.json: the Rankings' measures file, the real world's latest figures (batch LB1; the project's
spec-leaderboards.md 6-8). Written by tools/world/measures.py, read by ham.citybuildersim.WorldMeasures, checked by
WorldMeasuresCheck. It lives in the resources (src/main/resources/world/world-2025.json), UTF-8, under 1 MB. The game
never fetches anything; the file is rebuilt only by running the pipeline.

ONE OBJECT, two members: "meta" and "places".

META
  name        "world-2025"
  made        yyyy-mm-dd, the day the file was built
  year        2025, the board's year: each figure is its place's latest in the window
  window      [2015, 2025]: a figure older than the window's first year is left out (absent), never carried
  marks       {"3": {"symbol": "●", "name": "published"}, "2": {"symbol": "◐", "name": "a published projection"},
               "1": {"symbol": "○", "name": "the game's estimate"}}: world-1.bin's marks, the same numbers
  us_prices_2015_over_2025   the US's consumer prices of 2015 over 2025's: the CPI-U's annual averages divided,
               unrounded, so a real 2025 dollar becomes a 2015 dollar by this factor (the Rankings' real US$, D1/D29)
  cpi_u       {"2015": 237.017, "2016": ..., "2025": 321.943, "series": "CUUR0000SA0", "source": "bls"}: the CPI-U's
               annual averages for every year of the window, as the BLS publishes them; a figure in US$ of its year
               y becomes 2015 dollars times cpi_u["2015"] / cpi_u[y] (the ratio above is y = 2025's)
  sources     {id: {"name": ..., "url": ..., "licence": ..., "downloaded": yyyy-mm-dd}}: every source a figure
               names, each with its licence. Never the IMF's World Economic Outlook (nations 3.3).
  rules       {id: "one or two sentences"}: how the game made each kind of estimate (mark 1), for the drawer
  definitions {"oecd": "one sentence", "wup": "one sentence"}: the two city boundaries a region's def names
  measures    the 70 measures in tools/world/measures.tsv's order, each {"key", "group", "name", "unit",
               "arrow": "up" | "down" | "none", "money": true when it is in US$ and follows the Money toggle,
               "scope": "N" (nations) | "NC" (nations and city regions) | "Y" (only the player's cities rank, D10),
               "w": true for the ten Your world ranks (D15), "source": measures.tsv's spec, "note"}
  variants    {"gdp.k": {"of": "gdp", "money": "constant 2015 US$", "source": "wdi:NY.GDP.MKTP.KD"},
               "gdp.ppp": {"of": "gdp", "money": "current international $ (PPP)", "source": "wdi:NY.GDP.MKTP.PP.CD"},
               "gdp_pp.k": ..., "gdp_pp.ppp": ...}: GDP and GDP per person in the other two moneys (D14)
  counts      {"nations": 196, "regions": R, "figures": F, "estimates": E}: F the figures in the places' "f"
               members (a region's two shares are not counted), E those of them marked 1; the (i) prints them

PLACES, an array: the 196 nations in ISO 3166 alpha-3 order, then the city regions in tools/world/regions.tsv's order.
  id          a nation: its ISO3 (world-1.bin's); a city region: regions.tsv's three-letter id, which is never a
               nation's ISO3
  kind        "nation" | "region"
  name        a nation: world-1.bin's name; a city region: regions.tsv's
  band        the size band: the number of digits in the place's people less one (Halifax, 4xx,xxx: 5); absent
               when it has no people figure
  nation      a city region only: its nation's id
  def         a city region only: the boundary every figure of it follows, a meta.definitions id: "oecd" (the
               OECD's functional urban area, a city and its commuting zone, for a city whose share of its nation's
               GDP the OECD publishes) or "wup" (the UN's urban centre, World Urbanization Prospects 2025)
  share       a city region only: {"people": FIGURE, "output": FIGURE}, its share of its nation's people and
               output, each over 0 and under 1
  f           {measure key or variant key: FIGURE}. A measure with no figure for the place is ABSENT: never 0, never
               null, never NaN. A "Y" measure, and a measure whose source is "none:", is absent from every place.
               A city region carries only "NC" measures (and the GDP variants).

A FIGURE is a JSON array: [value, year, mark, source] or [value, year, mark, source, rule].
  value       a finite number in the measure's unit
  year        the year the value is of (in the window)
  mark        3, 2 or 1 (meta.marks)
  source      a meta.sources id: the source the value was read or made from
  rule        a meta.rules id: present exactly when mark is 1, saying how the estimate was made

THE RULES the builder writes (ids), and where each applies
  ppp-market      D13: a nation with no published PPP figure takes its market figure (gdp.ppp, gdp_pp.ppp), mark 1.
  world-1         a nation with no WDI figure in the window for GDP (each money) or people takes world-1.bin's 2025
                  path figure (WN0's own fills: the Penn World Table, Maddison, the UN), with world-1's mark; mark 1
                  carries this rule.
  share-people    a city region with no published output share takes its share of the nation's people.
  region-output   a city region's GDP in each money is its output share times its nation's figure of the share's
                  year: mark 3 where the share is published for a year the nation's figure is published for,
                  else mark 1 with this rule (the nation's latest figure times a share of another year). A region
                  whose output share is its share of people (share-people) has no GDP per person: it would only
                  repeat its nation's.
  derived figures (a ratio of two published figures of one source and year, a ten-year compound rate) keep the
                  source's mark and need no rule: the measure's "source" spec says how they were made.
