earth-2: the Earth data of CityBuilderSim

earth-2.bin is derived from the data sets below. Their licences allow this use; their credits follow,
each in the form its publisher asks for. It ships beside the exe as data/earth-2-ATTRIBUTION.txt.

  Licence: Public domain; may be copied, published, adapted and commercially exploited; the source must be acknowledged; no official status or endorsement may be implied; not for navigation (GEBCO, "Terms of use for the GEBCO Grid and derived information products"); the copy read, at CEDA, is under the Open Government Licence v3.0: contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0 (https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/)

Not for navigation. GEBCO's grid, and so this file, must not be used for navigation or for any
purpose involving safety at sea. Neither GEBCO, the IHO, the IOC, NASA, ESA, the HydroLAKES or GRWL
authors, the USGS nor Natural Earth endorse this game.

Where a source does not reach (marked in the file's meta layer as well):
  - South of 60 S (Antarctica and its islands; 10,800 1-degree tiles) ESA WorldCover has no data: the coast there is GEBCO 2026's elevation >= 0 as a whole-cell mask (no refinement), and land cover is marked "no WorldCover data" with the ice bit set.
  - The 30,141 1-degree tiles north of 60 S with no WorldCover tile (open sea, and 17,454 15" cells GEBCO calls land) take GEBCO's TID mask (ocean or not) for their coast; land there is marked "no WorldCover data".
  - North of 61 N and south of 56 S, and in any tile NASADEM has no heights for, the slope is an estimate
    from GEBCO 2026's 15" grid (the layer slope.est marks those tiles).

No borders are drawn; place names are for search only.
