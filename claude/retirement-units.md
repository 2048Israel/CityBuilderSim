# Retiring mills, mines and track

RT2 completes RT1's audit of the buildings that did not count in the unit
their sector's retirement rule used. A Bakery counts both bread and baked
goods in heads served. Worked-out coal mines count in raw mine nameplate,
including when preparation plants wash the coal into other outputs. The
railway weighs offered tonnes against standing track, so a shortage of wagon
sets or station handling does not conceal track it could sell.

The loss streak, spare-capacity threshold, headroom and gradual-sale limits
keep their existing rules. The three fixtures now cause the missing sales:
one of two Bakeries, two of eight fully washed longwalls, and two of eight
Freight Lines under either operating bottleneck. All fail with main's sector
code. The default city's timing and later path move; its final comparison,
the one re-picked money fixture, and the ensemble are in
[RT2 notes](../docs/queue/RT2-notes.md).
