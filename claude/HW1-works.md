# HW1 — works ordered for useful service

Essential works target full useful service plus the player's reserve.
Highways, streets, power, water, care, every warranted school, police and
cells share that floor. First useful demand is 30% of the smallest work's
nameplate capacity. Orders use the normal treasury, debt, cap and land paths.

Build cards and automatic orders share the sizing rule and component sum.
Each complete plan ends twenty years from today, including construction.
The comparison charges initial and later works, actual-use owned ground,
parcel purchases, vault top-ups, quoted financing, filled-post running and
repairs. It credits physical output restored and returned payroll taxes.
The reserve target sizes capacity. Physical shortages determine output
relief, including Health's sickness cap and other causes.

Current shortages rank by preventable output loss and missing share.
Higher-priority needs preserve funding ahead of lower-priority proposals.
Saved first-completion dates limit pending capacity's cover for operating
shortages. Faster useful work can advance recovery, and expiry causes a
fresh order. Prospective construction quotes preserve an unserved
essential's promise through automatic reserves, including depots promoted
to builders-first by the proposed order. Current shortages and the player's
construction priorities remain part of the actual schedule.
