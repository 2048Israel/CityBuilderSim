cells share that floor. Essential orders bypass their own payback veto.

opportunity land, whole-parcel purchase cash, foreign-vault top-ups,

payroll taxes reduce the complete plan's cost.

bond terms use today's market inputs. Actual monthly orders buy capacity
needed at opening. Current essential shortages rank by preventable
output loss, then missing share. An unfunded higher-priority service
keeps cash ahead of lower-priority proposals. Funding reasons survive
reloads and clear when automation is switched off.

expires during a shortage, the builder orders again. Faster useful work
can advance that first completion. A real scratch save/load and its
unbroken twin reproduce the same deadlines and later orders.
