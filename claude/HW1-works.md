# HW1 — works ordered for useful service

Essential works target full useful service plus the player's reserve.
Highways, streets, power, water, care, every warranted school, police and
cells share that floor. First useful demand is 30% of the smallest work's
nameplate capacity. Their orders use the normal treasury, debt, cap and
land paths.

Build cards and automatic orders share the sizing rule and component
sum. Each complete plan ends twenty years from today, including its
construction wait. The comparison charges initial and later works,
actual-use owned ground, parcel purchases, vault top-ups, quoted financing,
filled-post running costs and repairs. It credits physical output restored
and returned payroll taxes. The reserve target sizes capacity. Physical
shortages determine output relief, with Health's sickness cap and other
causes included.

Current shortages rank by preventable output loss and missing share.
Higher-priority current needs preserve funding ahead of lower-priority
proposals. An existing ask's borrowing permission and held cash are
priced separately. The actual capped quote supplies remaining coupons
and maturity.

Saved first-completion dates limit how long pending capacity covers an
operating shortage. Faster useful work can advance recovery, and expiry
causes a fresh order. Prospective construction quotes keep automatic
reserve expansion inside an unserved essential's promise, including a
depot promoted to builders-first by the proposed order. The model still
schedules current shortages and the player's construction priorities.
Funding and construction explanations survive a reload and clear when
automation is switched off.
