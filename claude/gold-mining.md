# Gold mined at home

GD4 gives the city a way to make reserves through production. Gold fields
follow the existing geology. Shields and fold belts get most of them, and a
shield field starts richer than a basin field. A mine mills a fixed tonnage
of ore. As its field's recovered grade declines, the same crew, reagent and
energy bill buys fewer fine ounces.

Gold's selling price follows GoldPrice in nominal US dollars. Rising
operating costs against a fixed mint par can squeeze the mine, and a rise in
gold's dollar price can restore its margin. Under Gold the mint accepts the
mill's offer at M and settles it with new money when the sector's sale
reaches cash. The metal enters the vault at that settlement. An offer already
accepted survives a later choice to leave Gold.
