# The households' books closed

Household savings now have a monthly identity beside the money audit's pools: the change in what
residents held equals receipts less payments plus the signed adjustments named in their own
settlement. Money paid in one press and settled in the next is carried between them. Migrants' money and household
share trades with the world also reach the balance of payments. A flow is recorded when it happens.

Care, schools, research, safety and transit now buy their upkeep from Business Services. With seats,
the sector keeps a quarter and imports three quarters; with no office, it imports the whole bill.
The foreign share is deducted from GDP because that upkeep is already in government spending.
The bank buys its operating upkeep through a separate input line: its import stays outside GDP's
imports because the accounts have no final-output term for the bank. The money audit and foreign
accounts still record the cash crossing the border.

At bankruptcy, the discharged share's savings repay its debt first. The bank writes off only the
uncovered debt, and savings above it remain the household's. The old rule destroyed a share of
savings regardless of what was owed. The separate question of discharging someone who could repay
is left to HL2.

Nine faults in household settlement are visible as named own lines, measured and bounded. HL2 will
mend them: unfunded sales, money split over cells too small to settle, the census's stranded or
created savings, restruck public wages and a bond's face no cell holds. The recommended calls are
that an unfunded bill is not a sale, and a household whose savings cover its debt repays it without
bankruptcy. The present batch closes the identity while retaining those findings.

The resumed build retains current main, including public patent imports, gold minting, noncash
valuations, HN1's trimmed mortgages and LM1-D's founding water. Its caused checks close the books
through those money boundaries. The combined default run closes all 2,404 months; its eight trace
files are unchanged by the LM1-D merge. Full measurements and publication status are in the
[HL1 notes](../docs/queue/HL1-notes.md).

The final ensemble completes all 80 cities on the merged model. Every monthly household identity
closes, with no money-audit failure or LongPlaytest finding. It scores 23/26, all ten hard criteria
passing, as does the published 0.9.151 baseline. All sixteen AUTO2 cities take off.
