# Home choice and typed rents

An empty studio does not answer a family's need for a home, and an unaffordable
large house does not earn the rent of a fully occupied building. RES3 gives the
landlords fourteen construction forms containing thirty-two comparable dwelling
classes. Each class has its own posted ask and observed rent trades. A pooled
price remains a descriptive reader of the actual leases.

The monthly census supplies the existing household shape, income tier and adult
stage cells. Their resources reserve food and recurring obligations before a
home is considered. A bounded allocation keeps suitable affordable incumbents,
then places as many compatible affordable movers as possible. Area, layout,
quality, actual garden space and explicit accessibility rank the alternatives.
Equivalent cells and offers share a rationed result in proportion; catalogue
order supplies no entitlement. There are no individual household or home records.

Emergency crowding and sharing remain visible. Sharing uses only people left
without a normal placement and a real remaining home their combined resources
can fund. Five working singles enter their existing shared household cell;
financial stocks follow the existing census transfer path. Unplaced demand
retains shortage and affordability reasons. Two households may also divide one
actual door and its one rent bill; household counts and door-equivalents remain
separate, and a host's bill falls when a guest joins it. Retirement can remove
only whole structures whose complete dwelling mix has enough vacant RE titles.
The monthly quote is not repeated
until a price equilibrium is reached.

The catalogue enters the normal paid construction path. Materials, work,
payroll, utilities, plot costs, queues and lender decisions all use the actual
recipe. A builder's prospective rent is limited by eligible demand after standing
homes and already funded sites have reserved their share. Vacancy produces no
rental receipts. R1g's selected wage-following residential scaler dates costs
through the existing recipe and research machinery; its unrelated demographic
and GDP work is not part of RES3.

Class asks, actual trades, staleness, assignments and sharing transfers travel
through the save file and load rebuild. They are monthly flows that closing
stock cannot reconstruct. Old incompatible saves are refused under the project's
old-saves rule, without conversion code. Owner-family seekers and title/loan
cohorts remain the explicit BO1-H and HO1 seams.
