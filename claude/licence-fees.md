# Licence fees on imported discoveries

A city founded the 1900 way pays the world's patent rent through the firm
running a discovery. A patented catalogue entry costs a quarter of its positive
monthly gain until twenty years after history's date. Locally found discoveries
and unpatented entries are free. Planners and fitting decisions compare gains
after that fee.

Production captures the bill before the firm chooses its next run or mix.
The pending ledger saves it for the next strike. Liability is production's
patent window: last-month bills are still paid after expiry, while later
production accrues nothing.

The payment is an imported service named IP charges. The firm's monthly
statement settles its cash, the money audit names the trade outflow, and the
foreign current account takes the same reconciled month. The saved statement
and the monthly history keep paid and accrued flows for later readers.
Standing planners subtract the captured per-building fee, saved through a
load without recomputing it from the next run or prices. Default-founded
cities keep their previous path.

works and berths: the treasury must pay for their public gains, rather than
