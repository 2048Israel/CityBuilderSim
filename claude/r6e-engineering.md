# R6e: GM crop history and the rows left without a calibrated step

The 1996 GM-crop discovery now changes the Grain Farm and Mixed Farm's
historical crop output. Their modern figures remain exactly 500 and 69.
Before E21 is known, each runs at that modern anchor divided by 1.22.
The research date is 1996; June is a placeholder because the introduction
source gives no month. The existing seven-year research lag and work rule
are unchanged, so a native local discovery can arrive before history.

The multiplier is an explicitly approximate crop-mix estimate accepted by
the orchestrator. Klümper and Qaim's 2014 synthesis reports 22% in its
abstract and 21% in the body, with different results across crops, traits
and countries. This is not a universal trait effect. The game changes no
livestock, land, jobs, fuel, seed or pesticide input. The existing PE20,
E10 and E21 changes add fixed modern-anchor differences; they are not
three compounded yield ratios. The source's CC BY 4.0 attribution and the
game's changed mapping ship in the existing world attribution file.

E21's patentability is the existing technology-fee estimate. Native rates,
term, local exemption and production-period capture remain unchanged; it
is not a measured seed royalty and adds no seed invoice. Causing fixtures
exercise both farms' positive production across the date, local research,
partial production and its actual marginal licence bill.

A native pre-E21 save of shipped format 59 exposed a physical load fault.
At May 1996 its fitted tractor choice has no missing-E21 flag. It loads
immediately at 69/500 instead of 69/1.22 and 500/1.22. The next chooser
repairs the figures, but a loaded city must already be right. The format
and oldest loadable floor therefore both rise to 60. The loader refuses
those older saves and names the build that still opens them. There is no
conversion. Current saves retain their choices and immediate figures,
then agree with the unbroken city at the next month.

The other engineering subjects retain their figures and have explicit
provisional publication records in [R6e's ship file](../docs/queue/R6e-ship.md).
E24 still waits for PW3. E27 has no measured incremental step beyond E18.
The desalination rows need a seawater date and a consistent heat/electricity
boundary; autosteer needs its affected fuel fraction; autonomous mine haul
needs its haul bottleneck; calcined clay needs its thermal/kiln mapping;
electric trucks need the dynamic charged-fleet model. Their measured
component effects cannot be silently applied to whole buildings. The
numerical evidence, licensing limits and exact prospective methods are
preserved in [the batch notes](../docs/queue/R6e-notes.md).
