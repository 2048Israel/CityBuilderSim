# The input price a works forecasts

A works chooses how much to make before the month's goods markets clear.
Its export decision compares the output's net export price with the traded
inputs needed to make it. A profitable decision needs both prices on the
current world, exchange and freight band.

The former input quote used the preceding clearing's local price whenever
local supply existed. A new band could make the input dearer before the next
decision while the quote still said it was cheap. RT3's seed-11 wire works
then bought rod at the new price and exported wire below that rod bill.

GDP1 prices the last observed supply and demand on the current band for the
works' input quote. Every sector uses the same quote through Sector's input
costing, including stockable output and captive-input chains. The production
decision still forecasts quantities from observed demand, and the export
rule still measures traded feed cost; standing payroll and other overhead
remain costs the works pays while idle.

The recorded clearing price and statements retain the actual transactions.
Unsold stock already enters the inventory term, and a later export draws it
down. The corrected decision stops the observed wire run before a feed bill
is incurred for its loss-making exports.

The author's decision, fixtures, paired replays, GDP readers and final checks
are recorded in [the GDP1 notes](../docs/queue/GDP1-notes.md).
