# Money on Canada's dates — R0f

A city founded the 1900 way now starts with its dollar held at par by its reserves. It floats from August 1914, returns to par in July 1926, and floats again from September 1931. Exhaustion ends a city's current gold episode early; replenishing the vault does not silently put it back on gold. The 1926 return is a new episode. Interest follows the world while gold holds and before the Finance Act; the government's manual Advance Rate opens in August 1914. March 1935 opens the Bank of Canada's remaining Money controls. These are dates, not discoveries.

The dated reserve rule uses the whole vault and never breaks by doubt. It is separate from the chosen Gold regime's metal cover, gold points and defence rate. The older automatic central-bank ledger also represents the government's pre-Bank money accounting.

Default founding retains its present Money mechanics. This remains opt-in with the other 1900 systems until R1d enables that founding. Old unfitted saves would otherwise load under a different monetary contract, so the save format and oldest readable format both rise to 53. R0f-notes.md records the fixtures, checks and the separate R0f-S reload finding.
