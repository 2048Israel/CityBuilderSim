Everything here is **verbatim**, in the file's own order, under the `##` and `###` headings it sat under (repeated above it so this reads on its own). Two kinds of thing moved:

- **Struck-through items, whatever their date:** a list item whose title is wrapped in `~~` (bold or plain), with every line under it to the next item at its depth or shallower; and the one `###` heading wrapped in `~~`, with its section. A live parent stayed in the project with its unstruck children; only its struck children came here.

The newest date mentioned in what moved is 2026-10-11 (the answers recorded in the moved CONFIRM lists). The project's `claude/todo.md` is the live list and holds the rest. The HTML comment before each moved item gives where it stood in the project's file just before the move (line numbers of that file) and, for a child, the parent it sat under; GitHub does not show it.
