# UX16 - buying on credit: how the gap is paid, and for how long

Jerus, 2026-11-27, about 22:15, through the desk chat: "the auto borrow aka when
you dont have enough cash and click buy land on credit, should also be updated
and show a few more options atleast no?", then "like aside from show more
options a quick (change duration) dropdown or something idk".

## What there is now

- **What every "on credit" button does.** It takes the gap past the cash onto
  the overdraft: the central bank's advances to the treasury, up to the
  ceiling, priced as debt.
  - The rule is Game's STANDING POLICY / UNTIL THE CEILING, and `buildFor()`.
- **The buttons:**
  - `BuildCard`'s `VerdictKind.BILL`;
  - "Build N on credit · $X" on the cards and on the order bar;
  - the Land office's `Look.CREDIT` buttons (`BuildScreen`, `LandScreen`).

## What to build

1. **At each "on credit" button, a choice of how the gap is paid.** Each choice
   shows its rate and its monthly cost:
   - the overdraft, as now, with the room left under the ceiling;
   - a bill for the gap;
   - a bond for the gap;
   - only what the cash covers;
   - "more…", which opens UX15's borrow screen with the gap filled in.
2. **A quick duration dropdown beside it:** overdraft, 3, 6 or 12 months, and 5,
   10 or 30 years.
   - The rate and the monthly cost change as it moves.
3. **The default stays the overdraft**, so a one-click order behaves exactly as
   it does now.
   - An old save and the auto-builder are unchanged.
4. **One model.** Every quote is the one the borrow screen and `DebtManager`
   give for the same terms. The button recomputes nothing.

## The checks

- **Each choice** issues exactly what it quoted.
- **The money audit** is exact to the cent.
- **The default** equals today's order to the cent: the same overdraft, the
  same price and the same month.
- **The auto-builder's orders** are unchanged on the playtest trace.
- **Stability.** UiSteadyCheck covers the choice and the dropdown.
