## The fault (W1, 0.7.99; `docs/queue/I14-notes/I14-todo-lines.md`; fixI14-notes §7)

Over a dense iron cluster `DistrictPlan` can leave a street piece walled in
by buildings and mines: `join()` (and `joinFromSmall()`) walks the ground
between pieces but never over a building, so a piece the buildings enclose
is never joined. Seen at MapCheck's dry place one cell south (359424,
371712), the x 10,000 copy, district (1, -1): 15,260 mined plots (23%) and a
50-plot piece the ground does not part, so PlanCheck's one network fails
there. No fixture stands there now; W1's clusters make such districts
possible in a dense city. The probe was `scratch-w1/probe2 W1Plan` (not in
the tree).

## What to build

- **A fixture that causes it** first (README's *The checks*): a PlanCheck
  section that rebuilds the district above, or a synthetic district whose
  buildings ring a street piece, red on main.
- **The fix**, the planner's to choose and to say why: lay the buildings
  so no piece is enclosed (a building that would close a ring moves to the
  next lot), or let the join take the cheapest building-free way round by
  the lattice, or, when nothing else joins it, drop the piece's street
  plots to ground. Every district keeps PlanCheck's one network.
- **Count** across MapCheck's and PlanCheck's cities how many districts
  meet the case, before and after, and print it.
- The plans' pure-function rule holds: the same district, the same plan.
