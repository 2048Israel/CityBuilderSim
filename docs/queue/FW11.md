## The fault (W1, 0.7.99; `docs/queue/I14-notes/I14-todo-lines.md`; fixI14-notes §7)

Over a dense iron cluster `DistrictPlan` can leave a street piece walled in
by buildings and mines: `join()` (and `joinFromSmall()`) walks the ground
between pieces but never over a building, so a piece the buildings enclose
is never joined. Seen at MapCheck's dry place one cell south (359424,
371712), the x 10,000 copy, district (1, -1): 15,260 mined plots (23%) and a
50-plot piece the ground does not part, so PlanCheck's one network fails
there. No fixture stands there now; W1's clusters make such districts
possible in a dense city. The probe was `scratch-w1/probe2 W1Plan` (not in
the tree).

## A second case, in a played city (PT1-M4, 2026-10-24)

MapCheck section 8's played city (the default playtest's rhythm) on PT1-M4's
model (the works department retired, the city founded with a Builders' Yard)
draws two street pieces in district (0, 0): 198 plots, and a 22-plot boulevard
remnant at x 371744..371751, y 344380..344384, in an iron-field blob (36 owned
field sites, 3 mined), walled in by the plan's own houses (district frame
x 48..55, y 65..79) and ground the city does not own. The ground the remnant
stands on was bought after the cell was planned (FX6's dated ground), so the
cell grew into it with houses and no street. `join()` and `joinFromSmall()` bar
only mined sites and buildings (`DistrictPlan.joinable()`, `groundJoinable()`),
so they find no way and mark the piece parted; MapCheck's walk crosses
buildings, as it should ("a piece buildings wall in is the plans' to join"), and
reaches it - **MapCheck 8 is red on PT1-M4, a known red pinned to this batch**.
FW11's fix should clear it (by moving the closing building, a building-free way
round, or dropping the remnant), and its count of districts should include it.

## What to build
