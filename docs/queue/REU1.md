## Expected source scope

the Real Estate card/pane in `ui/SectorScreen`, through `Kept`. References:
`IndustryView`, `IndustryPicture`, industryCard/IndustryPane. No economic formulas,
new transactions or individual-home nodes in the UI.

## Show

- Full building forms, their dwelling mixes/areas/layouts/quality, stock and on-site
  pipeline; distinguish physical homes from owned, rented, vacant and listed shares.
- Actual class rents, transactions/turnover and last-price staleness; asks and
  listing age; affordability/layout/vacancy exclusions and sharing/crowding.
- Actual capital, work, material, staff, utility, tax/upkeep, funded completion
  and receipts/expenses. Show quoted assumptions only where clearly labelled.
- Owner full recurring bills/common fees; RE unsold contributions; management
  service revenue and expense; common cash/arrears, including zero RE ownership.
- Private, included reserved, separately let and free shared parking, tariffs,
  paid revenue and unserved demand. Counts reconcile to S=I+L+V.

Use proportional/count-labelled aggregate pictograms and a precise legend, never
a tile per represented home/person. Keep nodes and scroll stable through monthly
redraw. Empty, mixed, parking-short, unsold and fully household-owned fixtures
must reconcile to saved model statements and inventories before visual review.
