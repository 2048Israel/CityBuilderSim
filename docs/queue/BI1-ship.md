### {next} — BI1, the Bank page's integrity cards relative to the book — 2026-11-20, ready, see docs/queue/BI1-notes.md
The Bank page's two red cards, "Its lines do not add up" and "Its two parts do not add up to its equity", and the Balance sheet page's "Not accounted for" line tested the books' residual against an absolute $5, so float dust lit them on a big city: a double's own step is .002 to .008 at 1e13, and KeptCheck's city of 4,820 months holds books of 1e13 and more. The test is the audit's now, a cent or a part in ten million of the figures' size, and it lives in `Bank` behind public getters (`sheetAddsUp`, `yearAgoSheetAddsUp`, `equitySplitAddsUp`; the dial `Bank.INTEGRITY_RELATIVE`) that the screen reads and recomputes nothing. On a small bank the floor is a cent ($0.26) where it was $5, so a few dollars out of a very small book now shows; past a $50M book the bound is over $5. `BankCheck`'s new section builds a small bank $6 out (the cards show), a book of 1e13 and more with float dust only (they do not) and the same book truly out by more than its bound (they do); with the old absolute test back, eight of its assertions fail. The default playtest's trace is main's, byte for byte (eight files, 2,405 months).
