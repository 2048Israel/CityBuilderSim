# HT1 custody: exact proposed owner extension

Historical STATIC proposal, accepted for implementation by root mail c483 on 2026-11-21. The custody extension is now being implemented on the same branch; runtime proofs and fresh review remain owed. The implementation checkpoint in HT1-notes.md supersedes the status statements in this original proposal. L7 remains its separate Pending owner item.

## One receiving owner

Add one Game-owned `EstateCustody` capsule, never a household/census cell, sector, ordinary TreasuryFund market book or rescue book. It contains fixed company share quantities, one instance of the existing FundLedger for its actual equity/bond accounting, a face map by existing live corporate-bond ID, USD quantity, idle car quantity, inherited loss carry, and pending cash entitlements. No person/estate list. Equity and corporate-bond issuer records expose its separate ownership; the capsule is not represented by silently increasing existing `cityShares`/`CorporateBond.city`, whose meaning is the fund. Actual treasury cash remains canonical in Game.cash; the capsule keeps pending/struck receipt counters rather than a second spendable cash pool.

Proposed entry: `HouseholdBalance.passOn` obtains the no-heir receiving route before any estate cash tax is collected, then calls `Game.receiveUnclaimedEstate(assets)` once. Determine that route with the same existing heir weights/counts, without changing their order. For a true no-heir estate, assess once and step basis as usual, but do **not** first classify its cash as tax: stage the assessment as a claim, transfer cash as unclaimed property, and consolidate the treasury's now-self claim after the asset receiver is installed. This requires a narrowly extended Deemed/estate routing parameter in the already-owned HouseholdBalance seam. It avoids both unclaimed-tax revenue and loss of the assets. Ordinary estates retain acquired debt-first/cash-first tax collection unchanged.

City-paper reservation is necessary: Debt.retirePart explicitly rejects `unsettledFace > 0 || settleDue > 0`. Never remove that guard to make a test pass. Available-household face/discount getters must exclude the reservation during the short capture-to-settle window; a household desk sale cannot consume reserved estate paper. Snapshot both Debt reservation fields and capsule pending amount through this window. Consolidation produces **zero cash**. The book difference between released issuer carrying value and the received holder book amount is a separately identified noncash debt-retirement result under the existing retirement/treasury-not-cash bridge, not a fabricated paid price. Linked/floating/serial schedules use the existing retirePart/shrinkSchedule contract, with causing fixtures for each needed type. No new Holder enum is necessary for this temporary reserved subset, and no existing ordinal changes.

## Cash, budget and audit edges

Propose one explicit **Unclaimed estates and receipts** cash revenue line in NationalAccounts/GovernmentScreen, with pending/struck Game counters. It receives inherited local cash and actual custody dividends/coupons/principal realizations. Noncash acquisitions, custody quantities/book values, foreign coupon cash paid home, claim consolidation and asset losses are separate disclosures/history, never tax receipts or cash created at a mark. Keep the cash/noncash distinction visible; a combined labelled market value is not cash.

At the initial cash escheat, MoneyAudit declares one DOMESTIC household-to-treasury inflow and removes that amount from both current LeftWith legs; it must not also appear as emigration. Equity/bond transfers are noncash holder changes. A future custody dividend/coupon/redemption is issuer-pool to treasury-pool cash, so adjust the existing **gross household/foreign** classification to exclude that receiver and do not invent an extra external inflow. Already-recorded coupon cash paid from the pending custody bucket similarly replaces the old household edge once, with no second issuer debit. TreasuryJournal records actual unclaimed cash and its budget inclusion exactly once using the already-budgeted-revenue convention; the bridge cannot count it again as an adjustment. Own-paper consolidation, receipt of USD and receipt of cars have no cash edge; any noncash debt result uses its existing not-cash bridge. No GDP/reserve equation change.

New-city reset clears owner and issuer counterparts together; order expiry does not clear custody. No save conversion or another prototype SAVE_FORMAT bump is proposed.

Causing no-heir fixture: establish a sole senior household and **no eligible working heir anywhere**, containing local savings, revolving debt, student debt, company shares/basis, tagged old claim, carried losses, USD, cars, own-city paper with nonzero holder discount, two distinct live corporate bonds, and a nonzero coupon already recorded under its cell. Cause its death via existing census/flow APIs. Assert debt priority; cash reaches treasury only under unclaimed-property classification; no capital-tax collection on the self-claim; new/old claims consolidate exactly once; every share, basis, loss, USD, car, bond face and pending coupon reaches the named owner. Assert Equity and each CorporateBond holder sum, reservation/private paper availability, post-settle actual issuer face/carrying/schedule reduction and zero redemption cash. Advance through a real dividend, recorded coupon payment and bond maturity; verify issuer cash, treasury cash and pending queues exactly once, with no foreign/fund spill, ownership-limit offer or forced fill. Save/load both before and after settlement/reservation and continue. Include splits/currency, genuine issuer extinction, empty-market persistence, and a mixed fixture with an unrelated real heir to prove the special route does not alter ordinary estates. No numeric formula-copy oracle or premise removal.
