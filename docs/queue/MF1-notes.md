## 1. Each fault: the cause, measured, and the fix

**1 and 2. Highways across buildings; works on the rail yard** (shots 2, 8, 12, 13; FX7's ★1, his call "Buildings move off"). *Cause:* FX7's rule, "a run laid later moves nothing": a box under a highway, track or yard laid after it stayed and both were drawn. On the playtest city's copy (m4,005) 587 of 12,125 drawn boxes stood on a run or a highway's verge, and `PlanCheck <save>` 9 failed 7 of 14 districts. Item 2 is the same case: the boxes on shot 13's yard stand on the plan's own yard ground, laid after them (the journal's records at m2,753, 2,926, 3,216 on the copy). *Fix* (`DistrictPlan`, BUILDINGS MOVE OFF A RUN): the plan lays the runs' records month by month before every event, departures included (`growRuns`), and the boxes a month's records lie across - or beside, for a highway (its verge, H5; FW4-C, ★1) - leave and are placed again as the same buildings (`moveOff`): their old cell first, then as any arrival, else on to the next district, the pair dated at the record's month. A box no run lands on never moves. *After:* section 9 green in 14 of 14; 497 boxes moved, 266 rebuilt in their district and 231 gone on (the copy is out of ground). The playtest's watch counts a box that left a run apart ("moved off a run", 0 in its windows) and flags nothing.

**3. The half roundabout** (shot 11). *Cause:* not a clipped disc but two whole discs half a plot apart, joined at a tile seam. Each tile read a crossing from a ring of street plots 4 wide, so a crossing wider than 3 plots on a tile's corner was read at a different size by each of its tiles; and a tile read its own plots as painted (a highway over a street erased it) but its neighbours' as the plan's codes. Unlike reads of one crossing: 26 on slot 7, 3 on the playtest city. *Fix* (`TilePainter.CROSS_REACH` 8, `TileRaster.Crossings`): the ring is 8 plots and every plot is read as the plan's code; 0 unlike reads after.

**4. The four-ring interchange** (shot 5). *Fix* (`TileRaster.loops()`): in each quadrant a leaf, a 270-degree loop (`LEAF_RADIUS` 2.4) tangent to both highways, and an outer connector (`CONNECTOR_RADIUS` 5.2); a trumpet one leaf; all inside the 19 x 19 reserve (★5).

**5. A wide road drawn as three** (shots 1 and 2: "like its a 3x14 road ... shown as 3 roads, with intersection on every node"). *Cause:* the painter gave each street plot its own bar through its middle; at half width (most of a road-poor city's: the plan gives every street half width first) the plot's other half stayed verge, so a band of plots was a lattice with a + at every plot. *Fix* (`TileRaster.bands()`): the mesh of every 2 x 2 block of street plots is filled, so a band is one carriageway; a plain + of one-plot streets is as it was. The plan is untouched.

**6. Square rail and dead ends** (shots 7, 12). *Cause:* the lines along the streets were the district plans' sidings (one per Rail Spur, to its works), laid plot by plot with no route: `TrackCurves` rounds only routes, so they never curved, no train ran on them, and two side by side drew as a ladder (shot 7's "thin double lines"). And at a T, where a siding leaves a line, `TrackCurves` took the line's own plots into the arc and left a gap, then a stub (the runs' sidings too). *Fix:* each plan siding keeps its corners (`DistrictPlan.sidingWays`), the map runs a train's route through them, rounded (`CityMap.planSidingRoutes`), and the painter draws the arcs; a siding keeps a plot clear of another's; at a turnout the line's plots stay (`TrackCurves.branchArm`). A siding a hopper unit train does not fit on (`sidingLeast`, 42 plots) is not drawn (★3). MapCheck 8c's iron city: 3 square corners and 58 ladder plots before, every corner an arc and none after; slot 8's two sidings were straight.
*The yard relaid* (FX7's ★4, "Into MF1"). *Cause, traced* (`MF1/MF1YardProbe.java`): not the runs. The model's Freight sector sells its only Rail Depot once its last spur is gone (`Freight.mayRetire()` keeps the last station only while a spur or line stands) and orders another 3 to 93 months later - 16 times in the playtest, 4 of them in the same month - and the map followed the count: the yard taken up and laid again on the same box 16 times, and the railway's whole net reset with it each time. *Fix, on the map* (`CityRuns`, a yard whose building left stays laid, idle): such a yard stays laid for `YARD_IDLE_MONTHS` (120) with no trains or wagons, the main line held; the depot asked again takes it back. Laid once and taken up 0 times after (16 and 16 before); the net laid once. The sidecar keeps the idle months (FORMAT 14; 13 and older read as before). The model's churn is queued as FD1 (★2).

**7. The outer rim** (shot 6). *Confirmed:* R7's packing - what the last district carries on, one box to an edge tile's corner, farthest tile first: a 0.96 km lattice over the city's far ground. Only the map reads it. The playtest at m4,000 packs 4,530 (3,259 homes); his slots 7 and 8 pack none. *Fix* (`CityMap.packAtEdge`): a packed building stands against a street of its edge district, else within reach of one, else with a street laid straight out to the first it meets (`LAID_MOST` 8 plots, drawn as an unmade street, the model's road unchanged); each tile filled before the next. *After:* every packed box within 4 plots of a street (median 1, none in the field); packed boxes moving month to month 11.0% of them a month pair -> 1.6%; the packing 66.7 -> 13.6 ms at m4,000. The key says "packed at the city's edge". A large works with no room beside a street is drawn smaller rather than whole in the field (★4).
*CL3's re-chaining:* settled already, by FX6's dated ground: MapCheck 2g's district bought second of five moves none of the 4,421 boxes after it (with the dating off, 1,016 to 1,179 move); the playtest's watch moved 0 in all 48 month pairs on main. Not settled: a city of more than 128 districts, where the chain is cut in bands of 128 and a district bought ahead moves each band's cut one on (1,315 of 1,491 boxes moved in the district that becomes a band's first, with the bands set to 3 in a scratch copy). His city has 93 districts. Under Pending (MF1-B).

**8. The coast.** (shots 3, 9, 10; after SL1, which made each terminal a real one of several berths and shared the lanes). *Cause:* the shore's rule, O13's: each terminal on owned dry ground at the water, its quay run straight out, wherever the coast had room - slot 8 had 106 terminals on 106 piers of their own (a quay of 9.4 plots on average), 92 of them in one chain along 23 km; slot 7, 50 along 16 km. *Fix* (`CityShore`'s `HARBOUR_*` dials, `CityMap` THE SHORE: `harbourSpot`, `openHarbour`): the first berthed work opens a harbour at the city's best shore, weighed over up to `HARBOUR_CANDIDATES` 8 shores by distance, exposure and shallow water (deep enough: `HARBOUR_DEPTH_M` 14 m, read `HARBOUR_DEPTH_AT` 10 plots out); each later one stands within `HARBOUR_REACH` (100 plots) of the harbour's works on a common quay (`HARBOUR_QUAY_PLOTS` 2); a second opens `HARBOUR_APART` (300 plots) from the first only when it is full, and no more (`HARBOURS_MOST` 2, his "one or two"); the rest of the coast stays beach. A harbour keeps off every spine arm's walk to the world (`worldWalks`, `WORLD_GAP`). Nothing laid moves; the harbour is worked out from the works, never saved. *After:* slot 8, 38 laid in two harbours of 22 and 16 works, each about 6 km, every quay 2 plots, and 69 counted short, drawn by their districts among the blocks (★6: the save still has the pre-SL1 counts, 91 Bulk Berths); slot 7, 41 in two harbours, 9 short; the playtest at m4,000 lays all 21 in two harbours, none short. The lanes leave each harbour as SL1's shared trunk. The shore's "came up short" mark is kept per type: one mark for all let a large type's failure stop every smaller one that still fitted (16 works laid where 35 fit). GatewayCheck's coast had its world line only because an old berth's verge stood on an arm's walk and read as the city's edge (the same coast with no works has none): its sections, and SL1's four coast fixtures, whose numbers were built for the old layout, keep it (`MapCheck.coastBeforeMF1`); rule-on twins hold SL1's exactness (65 searches and 5,577 shut-water asks, 0 differ) and timing (a month that lays one terminal 0.52 ms at 10 standing, 0.48 at 60).
