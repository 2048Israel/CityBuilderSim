## changelog
### {next} — OA1, the open ask holds an essential works for years — 2026-11-25, ready, see docs/queue/OA1-notes.md
Automatic building's open ask (D30) now spends the bond money it raised on the works it raised it for. The reserve
was taken out of an ask's proceeds, and once its sales passed the order's cost nothing was offered again, so the debt
limit's test never ran: in PW3's ensemble a Water Treatment Plant's ask held its raised money for 135 months with water
served at 26%, its earmark keeping every other order waiting "for Water". An ask's proceeds now come first, outside
the reserve, as a real city's restricted bond proceeds sit beside its operating reserve (GASB 54, GFOA); where the
month's bills drew them, what they drew is offered again under the debt limit, which withdraws the ask when it closes
(real proceeds are committed within six months, 26 CFR 1.148-2(e)(2)). AskProceedsCheck causes it from a town short
of water (17 red on the old rule); AuctionCheck's "bills draw the money" and WorksFinanceCheck's fully raised
premise are restated to that law.
