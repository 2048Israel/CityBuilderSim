# UX11 - Finances in the panel and the wide sheet

From UX1 (`docs/queue/UX1-notes.md`; `docs/studies/ux1/audit-3-sectors-government-finances.md`, its Finances
part and five worst problems; mockup 3 is Finances > The book > Every piece in the wide sheet). A page row:
each page gets its narrow form for UX2's panel, keeps every figure, and its wide tables open in the sheet.

## What it builds

1. **The hub's six doors at its top**, not its foot (audit 3, worst 4): The position, The book, Borrow at
   home, Borrow abroad, Money, The bond market, the city's fund; the path line carries the depth.
2. **The narrow form** of each page: the position (overview, debt service, home and abroad, your rate), the
   book, borrowing, money, the bond market (every issue, a bond's book), the fund (portfolio, search,
   activity, rules and cash): their figures in a row, one chart, the rest behind Wide.
3. **The wide tables** (mockup 3): every issue, the book's every line, the fund's holdings and activity as
   tables with a sticky first column, column groups, row shading and a totals row; a bond picked from a
   searchable table, not 12 chips out of 173.
4. **Figures that agree** (worst 3): the fund chart's axis and line against "+5.9% over 10Y"; debt service's
   headline one measure in every city; two rates of one name named apart; "the dial" meaning one thing.
5. **Settings drawn as settings**, not tabs (057, 063, 069); the fund's Pay in and Draw out above the fold;
   write-downs grouped, not one row each.
