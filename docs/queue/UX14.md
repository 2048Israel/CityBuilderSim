its picks, every one A):

## What there is when this starts

- **UX2** builds the console's shell: a low, semi-round band at the bottom
  centre, over the map, about 60% of the width and 90-110 px tall. A click
  folds it down to its centre.
- **Its centre is the clock**, which UX2 moves out of the top strip: the date,
  play and pause, the speed, and the month's ring.
- **UX5** gives the map's hooks (a need tinting its districts, a page lighting
  its buildings).
- **UX7** gives the build screen's tiles.

## What to build (★, the desk chat's recommendation after his two lines)

1. **The left wing is quick build.**
   - It shows the advice's top picks and the player's last builds, one click to
     place, with the same tiles as UX7.
   - A "more" button opens the build screen in the panel.
2. **The right wing is money and debt.**
   - It shows the cash, the debt and its years of revenue, the rating, the next
     maturity (what is due and when), and the rate the city borrows at today.
   - Its Borrow button opens Finances > Borrow in the panel.
   - Every figure comes through the getters Finances already uses. The console
     recomputes nothing.
3. **The map's lenses** (power and wires, water, each need's coverage, land and
   zoning, freight, the districts) go in a small button beside the zoom buttons
   at the bottom right. It opens a short list, and they use UX5's hooks.

## The alternatives, mocked up beside ★

- **B:** the lenses in the left wing, and quick build beside the zoom buttons.
- **C:** four semicircle gauges in the right wing, in place of money and debt:
  inflation against its target, unemployment, the budget's balance and the
  policy rate.

## The checks

- **UiSteadyCheck covers the console:**
  - it never covers a panel's foot or the zoom buttons;
  - folding and unfolding it keeps the map's place and zoom;
  - a month's redraw rebuilds nothing in it, and nothing in it grows.
- **The figures.** Every figure on the console equals its Finances getter, and
  the harness asserts that against the getter, not a number.
