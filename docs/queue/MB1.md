# MB1 - big-city map bands

Queued 2026-10-31: Jerus ticked "Big-city map bands", MF1-B.

## The finding (MF1-notes §1, item 7)

The spill's chain is cut in bands of 128 districts. In a city of more than
128, a district bought ahead moves each band's cut one on, and the district
that becomes a band's first loses its carry: 1,315 of 1,491 boxes moved,
measured in a scratch copy with the bands set to 3. CL3's re-chaining is
otherwise settled by FX6's dated ground (MapCheck 2g). His city has 93
districts; a 10-billion city 14,661.

## What it does

Bands that do not move with a purchase: a rank kept per district (the order
it was bought, which FX6's dated ground already records, so a new district
joins the last band and no cut before it moves), or a cut by area. The batch
chooses, says why in its notes, and keeps the map unsaved (worked out from
what is saved, as the harbour is).

## Checks

A MapCheck section that causes it, with the bands set small as MF1 measured:
a district bought ahead moves no box in any other band, and the old rule
shows the fault. MapCheck 5's month at 5B and 10B against main beside the
same load; no budget moved. Map only: the trace does not move.
