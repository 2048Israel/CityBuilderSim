# RES5 - Standing-home disposal and declining unsold asks

## Starts after

HO1 is on main, including common costs and the immediate financial-demolition
guard. Coordinate the central retirement/foreclosure paths with SD1 and credit.

## Expected source scope

`sectors/RealEstate`, `HomeMarket` listing/closing policy, central financial
disposal in `Game`/credit, ownership/common/parking books and saved flows/history;
home/credit/housing/money/save tests. It is not the generic scrap-message correction.

## Chosen policy

Existing ordinary/distressed financial exits create standing-home listings,
not salvage. Keep seller category, occupancy, exit intent, ask/age band, quantity,
book/debt sums and carrying obligations in bounded cohorts. RE buys no stock in
its active exit category and makes no same-clearing round trip. Normal investment
elsewhere retains HO1's net-rental-value test.

Initial monthly unsold-exit markdown is1%; test0.5/1/2% sensitivity and calibrate
with caused/played evidence. Apply to continuing stock separately from new inflows
and sales; currency redenomination scales the quote and tick. At the positive
monetary tick an unsold home remains held/maintained. There is no demolition
timeout, forced sale guarantee or capitalized proceeds without a buyer.

Tenant retention, common costs and debt-release policy are inherited from HO1.
Only funded actual closes change ownership, cash, collateral or observed trade
price. A voluntary markdown is not an automatic insured default claim. Estate/
outbound sellers retain obligations until actual closing, then mortgage first.

## Caused acceptance

Ordinary/distressed exits preserve structure/land/material; monotonically declining
continuing asks with no trades; fresh dearer inflow cannot disguise that record;
unfunded credit/common bills can leave stock unsold; no buyer demand duplication;
tenant purchase/refusal and vacancy; sale below rental value without self-bid;
short sale cash/lender/default distinctions; partial/last sale and manager funding;
snapshot/reload in marketing gives the same next ask/close. Profile bounded
listing/ask bands and solver work. Publish played time-on-market/clearance/carry
results and rate sensitivity; no fake transaction to make a graph move.
