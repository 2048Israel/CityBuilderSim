## changelog

### {next} — DM2, The wall ways dated in a journal in the map's sidecar — 2026-11-12, ready, see docs/queue/DM2-notes.md
The map keeps a dated journal of the plots cleared for street pieces walled
in by buildings. New ways displace only the boxes crossing their plots,
and released ways offer their ground to later arrivals. Sidecar FORMAT 16
saves those events and the dated units allocated to sites, yards and shore
works, so a returning unit arrives in its return month. FC1 is enabled
throughout MapCheck. The 4,000-month playtest has no findings, and all eight
default model traces match main byte for byte.
