# ET4 slice C renders

Written by `ham.citybuildersim.tools.RunRenders` (a Medellin city on the real Earth, earth-2, played 2000 months, drawn at 8 px a plot by the game's own painters). Not a harness.

- `et4-a-medellin-viaduct.png` - the window of 4 x 3 tiles (1,024 x 768 px, 8 px a plot) of the Medellin city with the most plots of its highways and railway on steep ground: each drawn as a viaduct (a deck, the highway cased in the deck's colour).
- `et4-b-tunnel.txt` - the run had no plot of the runs on very steep ground.
- `et4-b-fixture-tunnel.png` - RunWorksCheck's fixture tile at 8 px (256 x 256): a highway along the top and a track down the left, each across flat, steep, very steep and flat ground - the viaduct on the steep stretch, the tunnel on the very steep with its dashed line and a portal at each end.
