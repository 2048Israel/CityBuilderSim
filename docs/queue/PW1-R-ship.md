### {next} — PW1-R, PW1's review follow-ups: power billed on the kWh served — 2026-11-25, ready, see docs/queue/PW1-R-notes.md
Power is billed on the kWh the month served. The grid dispatched each month at its season, July's load about a fifth above the year's mean and April's an eighth below, and bought its fuel on those kWh, but billed every class the year's mean, so the utility's margin took the gap. A bill is now the draw at the ratio delivered, the season its book was dispatched at and the class's price; the prices stay a kW of the year's mean, which the works' choices read. The water works' power and the water's price carry the season too, and the Services screen shows the month's load the bills carry. A works' own generation is worth, in its plans, its draw replaced at its class's price and its surplus at what the grid pays the works for it, no longer all of it at the class price (SpreadPlanner.ownCosts). A saved struck book is held field for field through a save (SaveFileCheck); a save's fuel names are read through Good.byName, and the month between a dispatch and the statements it bills is said where the book is described. BuildCardCheck's POWER row is held to the grid's own figure and reads whether a wind or solar farm stands.
