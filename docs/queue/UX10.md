# UX10 - Sectors and Government in the panel

From UX1 (`docs/queue/UX1-notes.md`; `docs/studies/ux1/audit-3-sectors-government-finances.md`, its Sectors
and Government parts and five worst problems; mockup 2 is a sector's balance sheet in the panel, mockup 4
the columns alternative). A page row: each page gets its narrow form for UX2's panel, keeps every figure,
and puts its wide tables behind Wide.

## What it builds

1. **The sector list** in the panel: each sector a row with its kept-a-month and workers, the losing ones
   first in red, the sectors with nothing standing folded to one line ("13 sectors with nothing built"),
   not full cards (audit 3, worst 5). "Power 7%" and "handed over 28% of what was asked" in full-size type.
2. **A sector's books** (Operations, Income, Balance sheet, Cash & debt, Investors) narrow, as mockup 2: the
   statement as a two-column table that fits, the ratios without empty "–" pills, the equity waterfall as a
   bridge on its own scale, the Investors reached one way (its tab), the banner drawn once; on the map, the
   sector's works lit (UX5's hook).
3. **Government**: Overview, Revenue, Spending, Output narrow; the headline and its rows agree (Spending's
   13.8% against rows of 6.4%; the young town's "SURPLUS" beside "a year's deficit"; a deficit never green);
   GDP reached from the top strip's GDP as well as from Output.
4. **No overprint** (worst 2): Investors' "Fit ..." buttons beside, not over, the value-added column; the
   leverage gauge's labels apart; no pill past its card.
