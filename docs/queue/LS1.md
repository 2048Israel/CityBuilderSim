# LS1 - the labour share by sector, a study

Queued 2026-10-31, settling HD1-L the recommended way, on Jerus's "Take all
(Recommended)" (BP1 item 9).

## The finding (HD1-notes)

Luxury Retail's payroll is 1% of its value added (Construction's 5%), where
US compensation is about half of value added. A Boutique's 7 posts for 320
pieces is a real boutique's staff, so the gap reads as wages trailing prices
over a long run, not as Luxury's posts.

## What it does

A study before any post or markup moves: each sector's payroll over its
value added through the default playtest (m600, 1,200, 2,400, 4,000) and an
Earth run, against BEA's compensation of employees over value added by
industry (GDP by Industry; public domain), with where the gap comes from
(the wage's path against prices, the markups, the posts). A note in
`docs/studies/` with its prototypes beside it, ending in the batch it
recommends, or none. No model change in this batch.
