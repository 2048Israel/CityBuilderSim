# UX12 - the Bank, the central bank and Trade in the panel

From UX1 (`docs/queue/UX1-notes.md`; `docs/studies/ux1/audit-4-bank-trade.md`, its pages and five worst
problems; mockup 5 is Trade > The month in the drawer alternative). A page row: each page gets its narrow
form for UX2's panel, keeps every figure, and its wide tables open in the sheet.

## What it builds

1. **One alert, once** (audit 4, worst 1): the bank's "It is under the minimum" card leaves all 16 Bank
   pages for UX3's mark (UX3 moves it; this batch lays out what is left), and the page that measures it keeps
   its gauge; Trade's four cards in the young town
   become marks (two of them were one condition). The month's waterfall starts at the page's top.
2. **Empty pages folded** (worst 2): the central bank's Holdings with nothing held is one line, not 28 rows
   of $0; an income statement of "–" says "nothing earned or paid yet"; the central bank's nine tabs become
   the pages that have content (about two pages' worth today, more when CBK2 and CBK3 add theirs).
3. **The dials in one place, drawn one way** (worst 4): the policy rate, the target, how strictly it is held,
   the term-paper share, the treasury advance and the currency set where the central bank's page is, each
   as one control (a slider with its step, or chips, never both and a stepper); the key fact in figures
   ("the rule wants −4.6%, the dial stops at 0%, you hold 2.36% in deflation"), not a grey paragraph.
4. **Danger drawn as danger** (worst 5): the central bank's −$11.4T equity (−214% of assets) a red figure in
   the page's top row; inflation far off target coloured; leverage under its minimum red beside its gauge;
   profit not green beside ROE −405%; trust near its floor amber; a foreign funding share of 1% not a card.
5. **Trade** narrow: the overview, the month, what we trade, partners, the currency, the reserves; the
   partners and goods tables wide.
