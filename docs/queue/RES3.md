## Chosen rules

- Space/layout, garden, quality/amenities and available accessibility matter
  under the actual full recurring budget. Use existing stage/shape data; retain
  the adults-only studio rule. Normal suitability and emergency placement differ.
- Use the numerical prototype's area/quality score as a starting calibration
  vector. Family/garden and retired/accessibility terms use actual attributes;
  missing attributes supply no invented feature. Keep coefficients explicit.
- Primary objective is compatible placements. Coalesce genuinely equivalent
  demand/offer ties and split their outcome pro rata; deterministic rotated keys
  handle remaining incomparable ties. Prove that enum order gives no entitlement.
- Own one class quote/rent per comparable class. Port the existing lease-length
  log-price/inflation update and class-specific scarcity/glut/carry logic. The
  0.058 gross cost-return anchor stays an initialization comparator.
- One monthly quote/clearing cadence; no monthly dozen-root-solve equilibrium.
  Preserve substitution and one-count demand. Separate asks, last actual class
  trades, trade quantity/value and staleness. Pooled mean trade price is descriptive.
- Feed actual mix, land, materials, points, payroll, utilities, service regime,
  queue/carry and occupied proceeds to the existing builder/funding path. The
  18-month synthetic pipeline is superseded. Vacancy earns no fictitious rent.
