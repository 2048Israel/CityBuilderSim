# R1e — Price and save a new building's equipment at its order

R1d leaves R1b-R's shell-price question open. A new building pays the
template's whole cost, and its equipment is chosen at completion: an absent
piece cannot be discounted safely while the contract can still deliver a
different set of pieces.

Recommended, after QN1 (whose proposal value this extends): choose and save
the new order's equipment when it is quoted and accepted. Define the
template's shell and the cost in goods and work of each piece once, at the
prices the planners use. Freeze the accepted set in the construction
contract and deliver that set at completion; a discovery or a price change
afterwards is a new fitting decision. Keep preparation, tax, material
escalation and the site's finite work consistent with that contract.
Source the split of a template's cash cost rather than subtracting an
uncapped market fitting quote from it.

Prove it with orders for the same template with and without a piece, a
discovery while both are on site, a save and load mid-build, and the
builders' actual receipts and investment and VAT entries. Keep R1d's
no-refund scrap rule and its credit of what was paid. A save-format
decision follows CLAUDE.md's standing rule.
