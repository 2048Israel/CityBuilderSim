# R1e ? price and save a new building's equipment at its order

R1d leaves R1b-R's shell-price question open. A new building currently pays the template's full cost; equipment is chosen at completion. An absent piece cannot be discounted safely while the contract can still deliver a different set of pieces.

Recommended: choose and save the new order's equipment when it is quoted and accepted. Define the template's shell and the incremental goods/work cost of each piece once, at the same prices the planners use. Freeze the accepted set in the construction contract and deliver that set at completion; a later discovery or price change is a new fitting decision. Keep preparation, tax, material escalation and the site's finite work consistent with that contract. Source the split of a template's cash cost rather than subtracting an uncapped market fitting quote from it.

Prove this with orders for the same template with and without a piece, a discovery while both are on site, a save/load mid-build, and the actual builders' receipts and investment/VAT entries. Keep the no-refund demolition rule and actual-payment credit from R1d. Any save-format decision follows CLAUDE.md's standing rule.
