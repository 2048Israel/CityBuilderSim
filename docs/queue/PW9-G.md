# PW9-G — wires ready with the power

## The finding

REV-PW9 P1-b asked why the modern-equipment city's Substation opened at
m177 rather than main's m162. The final PW9 mends reduce it to m175, but
generation still arrives before the wires can carry it. The final run has
eight months below 95% energy served through m180, against main's five;
population is 10,471 against 8,218. This is a delivery/coordination finding,
not a claim that the source-priced period station should have fewer posts
or a different fuel bill.

Native dispatch m127 offers 21.083 MW, but generates only 7.2 GWh against
9.899 GWh gross asked. The founding 10 MW grid supplies all of it; offered
steam generates zero. The Substation is still on site. Final actual held
strings at pre115–118 protect College's quoted first completion; it orders
at pre119. Main has the same College crew guard at pre115, orders at pre116
and opens at m162. The earlier proposal's paired native trace separates
three months of order delay from twelve additional construction months;
the final construction interval is 56 months against main's 46.

AutoBuilder, ConstructionControl and SimulationEngine's crew/priority/debt
laws are inherited. A larger live queue and a different finance/growth
path meet those laws. No one-order-removal counterfactual assigns every
delayed point to steam construction. FC2-C's specific foreign/capped-site
question is not an established owner for this case.

## The law and the batch

**A utility builds no more generation than its wires can carry, ordering
the Substation and lines with it.** The final modern-equipment city's
31 stations behind the 10 MW wire are the red to cause. This row is queued
for a builder; it does not wait for Jerus's review.

- Cause a town with adequate offered generation, a funded wire need, and
  an existing protected school deadline. Prove actual monthly delivery
  before and after, with native commissioning and crew accounting.
- Make the 31-station/10 MW condition fail the law on current main. Require
  matching wire capacity to be ordered and commissioned with generation,
  or hold the excess generation order; assert the real delivery effect.
- Trace GRID's need date, order sequence, debt holds and site allocation
  alongside generation. Its forecast currently reads the present month's
  season in `BuildAdvice.supplyDemand(GRID)` even for a future `Ahead`;
  check the future date and latitude against the native dispatch curve.
  This is an inherited reader to test, not a proved sole cause of the slip.
- Coordinate the wires and generation before their real need. Keep the
  protected completion, staffing, construction-point, cash/debt and save
  laws; do not hide the effect by moving a fixture or inflating capacity.
- Check whether the existing 100 MW Substation row can serve an early
  town's size and lead before adding any permanent catalogue id. Source
  any size/cost/lead decision. Do not resize plants already saved.
- Compare default 1900 first, then the modern-equipment city (whose clock
  also starts in 1900); record delivered energy and cost as well as firm
  power. Run the ensemble if the default trace changes.
