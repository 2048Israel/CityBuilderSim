### {next} — HG1, an empty shop starves a town — 2026-11-28, ready, see docs/queue/HG1-notes.md
Jerus's new 1900 city ("did the inflation broke?", desk chat 11-28) had no shop in 79 months. Retail's staffing
gate counted only spare workers, and the founding's own posts kept the diploma band short, so every shop format
was refused. Meanwhile 97.5% of the town went hungry, the price index never based, and the top bar said "Nothing
needs you". A shop the town goes short of is now staffed at the city's fill, as every post of its tier is, but only
as many as close the gap its shops, even fully staffed, leave in the households' need; the 80% stays. The index
bases on rent and any outside good bought (meals, fuels, cars), never fees, and groceries join it after a year of
sales without a jump.
NEEDS YOU has a FOOD row, listed from 4% hungry and red from 20%, with the door that mends it (Build > Shops, or
the food promise). On his copy: shops at m81, shelves full by m86, the index based at m103, the first rate at m116.
HungerCheck proves each part.
