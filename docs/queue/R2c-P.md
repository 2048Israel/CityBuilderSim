Research-design section 2.4 applies to technology the city got from the world.
In a city founded the 1900 way, a patented entry used by the city's own works
or its berths pays 25% of its positive gain until twenty years after history's
date; local discoveries and unpatented entries pay nothing. The treasury pays
the imported service as IP charges, and the public planners count the gain
less the fee. The payment, money audit and foreign current account close in
the same monthly window, with the saved flow carried through DataSave,
HistorySave and the load path.

R2c covers private firms only. A berth's gain is public port savings, not
Freight's earnings; do not charge Freight money it never earned. Cause
positive public gains and actual payments in fixtures, including the expiry
and exemptions, and compare default traces with main. Coordinate shared
monthly, treasury, port and public-planner code with any in-flight batch.
