# UX13 - Policy, History and Rankings in the panel

From UX1 (`docs/queue/UX1-notes.md`; `docs/studies/ux1/audit-5-policy-history.md`, its pages and five worst
problems). A page row: each page gets its narrow form for UX2's panel, keeps every figure and dial, and its
wide tables open in the sheet.

## What it builds

1. **The alarms where the player looks** (audit 5, worst 1): the hub's four look-alike "cannot borrow"
   cards become one line or a mark (UX3); the real crises (87.9% ate less than a basket, no school, no
   pension for 23,669 seniors, nobody researching) are marks and each sub-page's first figure, not 9 px pills;
   recent decisions above the fold.
2. **Drawings that hold** (worst 2): the qualification ladder draws its bars in the big city; the pension
   waterfall draws negatives downward; "0 of 0" is "nothing owed", not "Covered 100%"; a red 0 only where 0 is
   the alarm; the rate line's labels apart; no codes in player text ("(E)", "Q11", "FERTILISER").
3. **One way to each area** (worst 3): the path line and the area's own tabs, one row of tabs at most; the
   area buttons, cards and strip links that lead to the same six areas become one; options drawn one way
   whether chosen or not; a button never jumps to another tab's area without the path saying so.
4. **Tables an eye can follow** (worst 4): "Who pays" with its rate beside its name (or wide, with row
   shading), bars on a scale that leaves no stubs, prose under a figure at most one line.
5. **No half-empty page** (worst 5): Fuel, Port dues, Currency reform, Zoning and Research's overview
   gathered where they are one panel each; Subsidies' 25 buttons a table with each one's figure.
6. **History and Rankings** narrow; their long charts and tables wide.
