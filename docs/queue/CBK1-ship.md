## changelog
### {next} — CBK1, the Bank tab's two banks and the central bank's page — 2026-11-26, ready, see docs/queue/CBK1-notes.md
The Bank tab opens on a choice (Jerus, 2026-11-26: "when you click bank you have two options, the commercial bank, and the
central bank"). The commercial bank keeps its pages, behind "Bank › Commercial bank". The central bank has nine of its own.
Its income statement runs from the window, the treasury's advances and its paper, less reserves, to the profit, the
remittance and the loss carried. Its balance sheet stands against a year ago, and money made and destroyed comes to the
change in M0 to the cent. Holdings shows the maturity ladder at face and market, with what it takes off the curve. The
other pages are inflation and trust, M0 against M2 with the vault, history, and Policy's own dials. Finances › Money and
Government open them. The figures are written beside the month and read by nothing in it, so the playtest trace's eight
md5s are main's; they are saved at the end of its own array, so an old save reads them as zeros. CentralBankStatementCheck
holds the statements to its figures, and KeptCheck 104-106 the pages.
