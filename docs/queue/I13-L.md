the mortgage lender's test, and the Ore card for every ore." Two notes I13

## 1. The landlords' card asks the mortgage lender

The build card asks every order the INTEREST gate except a landlord's home,
which the mortgage lender's test decides (`BuildCard`'s banner at the INTEREST
gate; `Game.buysOnMortgage()`, THE LANDLORDS' MORTGAGES; `Mortgage.decide()`).
So the card shows no last gate for a landlord's home, though the model has one.
C1's note: a door on `Game` round `Mortgage.decide()`, as `Game.interestTest()`
is the door for the INTEREST gate.

- A public door on `Game` that returns the lender's verdict and its figures for
  one landlord's home, from the same call the order makes, so the card and the
  order cannot disagree.
- The card shows it as the home's last gate, in the lender's words: what it
  lends, the payment against the rent, the verdict.
- A harness section in `BuildCardCheck`: a fixture whose home the lender
  refuses, and one it accepts, caused by the inputs; the card's verdict equals
  the order's in both. `BuildCardCheck`'s "a landlord's home has no interest
  gate" stays true.

## 2. The Ore card for every ore

Land office > the "Ore and oil" card is iron's alone: `ui/LandScreen.ore()`
builds its `Ore` record from iron, its tooltip says "Iron mines are built under
Build > Industry", and "ore that nothing is digging" is iron's flag
(I6's note). The materials batches added coal, copper, stone and the forest.
`ExtractionSector` already words undug ore for each resource
(`ExtractionSector.java`, "under this city that nothing is digging").

- The card's figures come from a public model getter for each ore the city's
  ground holds, never computed in the screen (CLAUDE.md, "A screen section").
- One line for each ore: its tonnes and the mines working it. "Nothing is
  digging" is shown for each ore that has a deposit and no mine. The tooltip
  and the click go to the industry that digs it.
- A harness: a fixture city with a coal deposit and no coal mine shows coal's
  "nothing is digging"; with a mine, it does not. The same for iron, so iron's
  words are unchanged.

No save change expected. It is a screen batch, so it is checked by eye on the PC
at 1,389 x 868: a city with two ores, one dug and one not.
