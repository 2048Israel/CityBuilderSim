# KS1 — KeptCheck's fixtures cause their conditions

## Scope and calls

This run handles the fixture assertions assigned to KS1 in known-reds. The
remaining QUEUE row, including KT1 and the timing work, belongs to KS1-rest.
Only harness source changes. Any reproduced screen defect keeps its assertion
and receives a queued screen repair in the ship file.

- ★ Supply a sawmill through BuildingManager and hold it through a real month.
  Check IndustryView.drawn(), which requires standing works and a counted
  month. A treasury auction and available ground were incidental dependencies
  that could prevent this screen fixture from existing.
- ★ Read the expandable income note when its choice executes. The former
  cached note was chosen before eleven other month-advancing choices, so its
  details could have disappeared before it was opened.
- ★ Hold the sectors' plant investment during their reuse measurements,
  preserving any holds already present. At month 575 the uncontrolled probe
  added Copper Refining's first picture, making 111 nodes and retaining only
  70.2%. The held fixture retained at least 97.4% across the same operations
  choices and passed the whole sectors section. Monthly production, trading,
  wages, statements, every page choice and the original 90% floor remain.
- ★ Settle an explicit dividend into a rescue holding and the fund's ledger
  after the real month lands for the Portfolio animation fixture. Its amount
  moves the rounded worth. Waiting for the shared city's ordinary dividends
  did not cause either condition. The speed limits and animation assertions
  are retained.
- ★ Supply a long-dated debt line before Government's debt-fold choices. The
  fold's premise is outstanding paper through those choices. Selling all six
  kinds into whatever market the shared city has reached could leave none.
