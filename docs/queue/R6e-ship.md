### {next} — R6e, GM crop history — 2026-11-22, provisional, see docs/queue/R6e-notes.md
GM crops enter the Grain Farm and Mixed Farm's history in 1996. Before E21 is
known, crop output is the unchanged modern 500/69 divided by 1.22, an approximate
crop-mix estimate with its source limits and CC BY 4.0 credit disclosed. PE20,
E10 and E21 add fixed-anchor differences; livestock, land, jobs and inputs stay
unchanged. Native patentability is an estimate, not a measured seed royalty.
SAVE_FORMAT and the load floor rise to 60: pre-E21 choices in format 59 load
wrong crop figures immediately, so older saves are refused without conversion.
The other engineering figures remain unchanged, with their exact evidence and
model prerequisites recorded below; E24 still awaits PW3 and E27 an E18 comparison.
