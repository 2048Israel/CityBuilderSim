# UX15 - a new borrow screen: fast, every option in view, the quote live

Jerus, 2026-11-27, about 22:15, through the desk chat: "not the old borrow, a new
much better borrow screen, that shows the info, but is also very intuitive and
easy to borrow fast, yet all availbe options iwth icons and stuff".

## What there is now

- **The page.** Finances > Borrow: `FinancesScreen`'s `BORROW_PAGES`, "At home"
  and "Abroad".
- **The form and the quote.** The `borrowAsk` form, and the callable quote's
  line.
- **The model.** `DebtManager.offers()`, the auction's price and the callable
  quote. The new screen reads the same ones.

## What to build

1. **Fast.** One amount, one term, and one click to Borrow.
2. **Every option in view, by icon:**
   - bill, bond, serial or amortizing;
   - floating, linked and callable;
   - at home or abroad, and in which currency.

   The icons are UX6's set, in today's outline style.
3. **The live quote beside it, moving with the choices:**
   - the rate, the monthly cost and the total interest;
   - what it does to the debt's years of revenue and to the rating.

   Every figure comes through a getter, and the screen recomputes nothing. If
   a figure has no getter today, the model gains one, with a check.
4. **Presets**, such as "cover the gap" and "a year of the deficit". Their
   amounts come from the model's getters.
5. **The rarer settings folded away**, one click from view.
6. **One screen, two doors.**
   - Finances opens it, and so does the bottom console's Borrow button (UX14).
   - From the console it opens straight over the map, and closing it returns
     to the map.
   - If UX14 has shipped first, point its button here.

## The order of work

1. **The mockup first.**
   - Draw the screen and one or two alternatives over the real map with
     `ui/PagePictures`, as UX1 did.
   - Mail the desk chat the pictures' folder; it shows them to him.
2. **Then build to the recommendation.** Nothing waits on him: if he picks
   another before the build, build that.
3. **Before and after pictures** by `ui/PagePictures`.

## The checks

- **The quote.** Every option's quote on the screen equals `DebtManager`'s for
  the same terms, asserted against the getter, not a number.
- **The issue.** It books exactly what the quote said, and the money audit is
  exact to the cent.
- **Every option.** Each one today's page offers can still be issued from the
  new screen.
- **Stability.** UiSteadyCheck covers the screen.
