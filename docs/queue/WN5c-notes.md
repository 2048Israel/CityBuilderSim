## Design and thread ownership

★ Move measurement, retain geometry, price in the current month. LandMarket separates immutable measured ground and seller parts from pricing. MapView supplies Picks a dedicated low-priority daemon executor and Platform.runLater. An owner-thread request copies the office through quoter(), reserves draft ids, and clears the old shelf; the card says Pricing the block... and offers no purchase until completion. The worker measures only its copied office/CityLand and its private draft cache. Shared LandSellers and world assets retain WN5b’s existing thread-safe read contract. Worker results publish through the FX dispatcher; only FX accepts them, changes live drafts, computes current-month prices, installs landmass linkage, or changes Picks and UI nodes. The request’s cancellation flag alone is volatile across threads.

★ The month stays synchronous and in its existing order: it prices retained unowned ground with this month’s ground price, US price level and seller factors. Equal unowned plot counts prove unchanged geometry because ownership only grows; unowned resource fields cannot be extracted. A purchase that changes a screen draft drops it for worker remeasurement; disjoint purchases retain it. A result whose land, grid, ownership count, sellers or linked-set version changed is rejected. The office also rejects any superseded or already accepted request. A month passing during pricing is allowed: FX prices the returned geometry at acceptance using the current month, so no stale price lands a month late. Game.nextMonth still delegates to SimulationEngine.simulateMonth; neither month implementation or HW1 file was edited.

Headless model/harness Picks remain synchronous. Their draft geometry is retained too, so the monthly seller walk is removed there without changing prices or month order.
