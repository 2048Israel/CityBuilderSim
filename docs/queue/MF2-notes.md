**WN3-M(a), the one rail yard dealt by district order (fixed).** *Cause:* `CityMap.yardsOf` dealt the runs' yards to the first district in `districts` order counting the yard's type, wherever the yard stood; a Rail Depot arriving in a district earlier in that order took the yard's share, the district that stood the yard drew its own depot as a box at its dated place, and the boxes after it moved. *Fix:* each yard is dealt to the district whose frame holds its ground (`yardFrame`, `frameHolding` - `holdsMost`'s rule, the one that gives a run's works to a plan) when that district counts one of its type not yet dealt; any left over by the old order. MapCheck 20 (`YardCity`, two districts and no Game): on main the arrival is not drawn in the district ahead and 7 boxes of the yard's district move and 7 appear; 0 and 0 after, the arrival its district's one new box. *Left:* a yard whose frame's district counts no building of its type is still dealt by order (keeping it would need the deal saved).

**WN3-M(c), the undated interchange reserve (settled by UP5).** MapCheck 2h is its fixture: a reserve arriving over a built district moves off it the boxes it lies across or beside the highways laid with it and no others, its undated control moving more. No code.

**Bergen 1, bridges as staircases (fixed, `TileRaster`).** *Cause:* the deck pass laid a bar across every plot of the run; on a 45-degree stretch the carriageway is the band along the line (`diagBand`), so the deck was a staircase of plot-wide bars beside it, highway and railway alike. *Fix:* a 45-degree bridge's deck is that band, `deckAcross()` wide (the cross-section and a pixel either side), and the water between two steps is filled as `diagOpen` fills the carriageway's notch (`deckStepAt`, `deckOpen`). Straight bridges are untouched. JunctionCheck 22: on main, at 4 px, 36 of 180 deck pixels lie farther from the line than half the deck and a pixel (the railway at 8 px, 432 of 1,092); none after.

**Bergen 2, the broken cloverleaf (fixed, `DistrictPlan`, `TileRaster.loops`).** *Cause:* not the loops' tangency - on a clean crossing at 6 to 9 px every leaf and connector touches both carriageways (JunctionCheck 23's clean case is green on main). The loose arcs were an avenue's street plots: a reserve laid after the avenue (UP5's dated reserves) left every avenue plot under it a street (FX7's "a street under a later run stays", never looked at for the avenue), and `loops()` drew only on open plots, so the avenue cut each ramp in two. *Fix:* the plan takes up an avenue under a reserve laid after it (DistrictPlan, NO AVENUE THROUGH AN INTERCHANGE'S RESERVE LAID AFTER IT: `rsvNote`, `streetsOffReserve`, `avOwn`), the avenue ending at the reserve's edge on both sides and counted as not laid there, as the same reserve from the start counts it; and the ramps are drawn over any street left in the reserve (a cell's own, which FX7 keeps). PlanCheck 19: on main 148 avenue plots in the box at step 8 and 100 at step 20; 0 after. JunctionCheck 23: on main 14 curves for 8, 11 not touching both carriageways, at 6, 7 and 8 px; 8 whole after.

**Bergen 3, the rail V-turn to a dead end (fixed, `CityRuns`).** *Cause:* `CityRuns.step()` picks the way at the sea from the arm's home heading +-1, not from the heading it is on, so an arm already turned to home + 1 took home - 1 at the next sea: a V of 90 degrees whose second leg ended in empty ground. *Fix:* `RAIL_CORNER` (1): a railway arm takes no way more than 45 degrees off its heading; where that is the only way, it waits.
