Deere's own release would not open.
- Other makers (CNH, AGCO, Kubota): dated target not found (searched). AGCO sells autonomous tillage by subscription, no date (snippet).
effect targets:
- Deere: no operator-hours or acres-per-worker figure found; labour shortage is the stated motive [1][3].
- Measured/stated cases: Kentucky grower, 2,000 acres planted by a retrofit on a 2019 tractor: two workers (seed runner, tractor operator) moved back to harvest last fall; planting cost $828 an hour against $2,800 (his estimate, not measured); assumed 7 acres an hour, 19 days of 16 h = 304 h [4] page. (calc) 2,000 acres / 304 h = 6.6 acres an hour.
- Purdue paper (podcast, 2 Feb 2026): model assumes 10% human intervention ("six minutes of every hour"), about 18 tractor hours a day, 80% of a human operator's efficiency, $40,000 a power unit, $3 an acre fee; break-even hired-labour rate about $45 an hour (ideal) or about $140 (realistic) against $30 assumed [5] page. (calc) at 6 minutes an hour, one supervisor could mind up to about 10 machines (model assumption, not a result).
- Deere kit about $40,000-45,000 plus $10,000 a year [6] snippet.
slips: Deere's 2030 date unchanged 2022-2025; wording moved from "planned" (2022) to "demonstrate" (2024).
