### 3. Pension ages fixed by statute past 2030

dated targets:
- Denmark: 68 in 2030 (set 2015), 69 in 2035 (set 2020), 70 in 2040 (passed 22 May 2025); "certainty for the next 20 years, to 2045" [1] page. Vote 81-21 and "born after 31 Dec 1970 retire at 70": snippet [2] (the release names no birth years).

Outcome due by March 2029; the Work and Pensions Committee asked for the report by July 2026; changes need 10 years' notice [3] page. Report published: not found.
- OBR, July 2026: baseline has 68 in 2039 (para 2.16), described as "the Government's stated policy that the rise to 68 will happen in 2037-39", then 69 in the 2070s [7] page. Treasury, quoted by press: "The law remains to increase the state pension age to 68 in 2044" [3] page. The two conflict; statute says 2044-46.

effect targets:
