### 1. Tuberculosis vaccine M72/AS01E, and WHO's End TB targets
today: Phase 3 NCT06062238 (Gates Medical Research Institute; Wellcome and the Gates Foundation fund it): 20,080 enrolled, start 2024-03-05, active not recruiting, 54 sites in five countries; the primary analysis follows 110 TB cases [1][2][4]. No result yet.
dated targets:
- Registry: primary and study completion 2028-04 (estimated); primary outcome lab-confirmed pulmonary TB in the IGRA-positive cohort up to month 49; updated 2026-08-28 [1] page.
- Gates MRI "expects to have results by 2028 (if not sooner)" (personal communication, in a TAG report of March 2025) [3] page (PDF). At launch: "up to five years to complete the trial", then analysis and submissions [2][5] page.
- Licensure date from Gates MRI, WHO or a regulator: not found (searched Gates MRI's launch, enrolment and manufacturing-agreement pages, Wellcome, WHO). A 2026 modelling paper assumes approval in 2029: its planning assumption, not a target [6] snippet.
- WHO End TB Strategy vs 2015: 2030, new cases -80% and deaths -90% [7] page (page prints no baseline year); 2035, incidence rate -90% and deaths -95% vs 2015 [8] page. Milestones: 2020 deaths -35%, 2025 deaths -75% [9] page.
effect targets:
- M72 phase 2b: "approximately 50% protection (13/1626 versus 26/1663)" over three years, HIV-negative adults already infected [2][5] page; 50% (95% CI 2-74%) [10] page. (calc) 1 - (13/1626)/(26/1663) = 48.9%.
- WHO actuals: deaths 29% below 2015 in 2024 (1.23 million) vs the 75% milestone [9] page; incidence about 12% lower vs a 50% milestone (snippet, https://www.cidrap.umn.edu/tuberculosis/who-cites-progress-against-tb-globally-warns-about-reduced-funding). 2024: over 1.2 million deaths, 10.7 million ill; 18 vaccine candidates in trials, 6 in phase 3 [11] page.
slips: End TB 2025 milestone missed: deaths -29% vs -75% (calc: 46 points short) [9]. Enrolment finished 11 months early [4] page; the 2028 dates have not moved. 26,000 planned participants cut to 20,000: carried (snippet).
