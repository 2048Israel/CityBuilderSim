### 2. Fusion power
today: ITER (Cadarache) is operating to "Baseline 2024" (Dec 2025 council) [1]. Commonwealth Fusion Systems SPARC (Devens MA): first of 18 superconducting magnets installed Jan 2026; no plasma yet [2][3]. Helion Polaris reported a private-sector record temperature and measurable D-T fusion on 13 Feb 2026; Orion site work began Jul 2025 at Malaga WA; the reactor itself not yet being assembled; state radioactive-materials licences for Orion reported Jun 2026 [4]. China's BEST (Hefei) in final assembly [5]. No fusion machine has yet made net electricity.
slips: ITER vs 2016 plan: first plasma 2025 became start of research operation 2034; full magnetic energy 2036 (3 years later); deuterium-tritium 2039 (4 years later); about EUR 5 bn extra for the ITER Organization, still under member review [1]. SPARC first plasma: 2026 earlier, now 2027 (Fortune, Jan 2026) [3]. EUROfusion DEMO: older roadmaps had it running early 2040s; now 2050s, a trade report headlined "delayed past 2050" [6].
projected: CFS: SPARC net energy (Q>1) 2027 (CFS page) [2]; ARC, 400 MW, Chesterfield County VA, "early 2030s", Google to take 200 MW (announcement date not shown) [3]. Helion: Orion 50 MW to Microsoft by 2028 (Helion/Microsoft, reaffirmed Feb 2026; critics say unproven) [4]. UK STEP (West Burton): first operation 2040, at least 100 MW net electricity (UKAEA/STEP site); government funding headline GBP 2.5 bn [7]. China BEST: completion end of 2027 (Xinhua via NEI), design Q = 1-5 [5]. EUROfusion DEMO: 2050s (exact date not found) [6]. ITER: D-T 2039 [1].
effect: no commercial figures exist. ARC 400 MW; Orion 50 MW; STEP at least 100 MW net; BEST burning plasma Q 1-5. Capex, capacity factor, cost per MWh: not found in any source read.
grade: speculative for power to a grid before the 2030s (every date has slipped or rests on unbuilt plant). SPARC Q>1 2027 and BEST burning plasma 2027-28 are "likely" as physics milestones, not power.

### 3. Enhanced geothermal
today: Fervo Cape Station, Beaver County UT: first power from the first 33 MW GeoBlock announced 24 Sep 2026; Phase I about 100 MW = three 33 MW GeoBlocks; first block's contract date by 1 Oct 2026, other two by 1 Jan 2027; Phase II 400 MW (eight 50 MW blocks) under construction for 2028 [1]. A 30-day Project Red test gave 63 L/s and 3.5 MW from one well [2]. Eavor Geretsried (Bavaria) closed-loop: first power to grid about Dec 2025 [3].
slips: Eavor had targeted power in the first half of 2025 [3]; first output was roughly 0.5 MW against a design of 8.2 MWe plus 64 MW-thermal for district heat (one analysis; no 2026 ramp figure found) [3]. Fervo's own earlier dates for Cape: not found.
projected: Fervo: Phase II commercial operation 2028 (company, Sep 2026) [1]. DOE: next-generation geothermal about 90 GW in the US by 2050 (up to 300 GW if storage develops); drilling advances to reach an average $60-70/MWh by 2030, then the Enhanced Geothermal Shot target $45/MWh by 2035 (DOE, launched Sep 2022) [4][5].

Output per well 3.5 MW (Project Red, measured); a claim of over 10 MW per well at Cape is only in secondary sources, treat as unverified [6]. Cape Phase I debt $421 m non-recourse for about 100 MW (Mar 2026); capex per kW, today's $/MWh, capacity factor: not found. Eavor: 8.2 MWe design for four loops [3].
grade: firm for Fervo Phase I (running, first block in commercial operation per snippet); likely for the 2028 Phase II and the DOE price path; speculative for closed-loop (Eavor) at scale.

### 4. Perovskite-silicon tandem solar
today: Oxford PV shipped the first commercial tandem modules (72-cell, 24.5% module efficiency) to a US utility project in Sep 2024, made at Brandenburg an der Havel, Germany [1]. Lab records: LONGi 35.5% small-area tandem cell, verified by ESTI, announced 14 Jul 2026; larger cells lower: 34.3% on 261 cm2, 32.2% on 274 cm2; tandem modules 31.4% and 29.4% in the efficiency tables [2]. Oxford PV holds a 26.9% record for a 60-cell module; a 25.6% shingled module with Fraunhofer ISE (Jun 2026) [3]. Trina Solar showed a 907 W tandem module at 29.2% at SNEC 2026 (trade blog) [4].
slips: no earlier Oxford PV volume target found. One trade report says LONGi launches the technology 25 Aug 2026 with mass production from Oct 2026, but it is unclear whether that means tandem, so treat as unconfirmed [2].
projected: pilot lines at several makers aiming at commercialisation in 2028-2029 (a 2026 trade review, weak source) [4]. Oxford PV gigawatt-scale ramp through 2027: only in a low-quality blog [3]. NREL's view: commercial viability turns on efficiency and production capacity [5].
effect: module efficiency 24.5% (shipped) to 29.4%-31.4% (record modules) against ordinary silicon modules, whose efficiency I did not source this session. Measured outdoor lifetime: not found; a 2024 review with NREL authors judged tandems not yet reliable enough to prove commercial viability [4]. $/W, GW capacity, degradation rate: not found.
grade: likely for 2028-2030 mass production; speculative for lifetime parity with silicon (no field data found).

slips: Cambridge was due online 2023, then late 2025; now commercial operation targeted end of 2026 [1].
projected: CATL: deliveries in China from Sep 2026, global from Jun 2027, about 1 GWh shipped by end of 2026; Naxtra EV cell in mass production by end of 2026 (CATL, 2026) [3]. grade: firm for vanadium flow at 1 GWh (running); likely for sodium-ion at GWh scale (dated shipments) and for Cambridge iron-air by end-2026 (slipped twice); speculative for the DOE $0.05/kWh target.
