### 1. Generative AI assistants at work
today: Census BTOS, wave ending 3 May 2026: 19.8% of US firms used AI [1]. 37% of firms with 250+ staff, 32% with 100-249, under 20% of firms with 1-4 staff; information 39.7%, finance/insurance 33.9%, retail about 14% [1]. The question was widened on 17 Nov 2025 from "in producing goods or services" to "in any business function", so the series has a break [1]. A press write-up of the 5-18 Oct 2026 wave gives 24.6% (unverified) [2].
slips: Gains shrink outside the lab. METR randomised trial, 16 experienced open-source developers, 246 tasks, early 2025: AI made tasks 19% slower while the developers believed 20% faster [3]. Denmark (Humlum and Vestergaard, admin data plus survey of about 25,000 workers in 7,000 workplaces, 11 occupations): no significant effect on earnings or recorded hours, confidence interval rules out more than 1%; self-reported time saved about 3%; 3-7% of the gain reaches pay [4]. Dell'Acqua et al.: on a task outside the AI's frontier, correctness fell 19 points [5].
projected: Acemoglu (NBER 2024, Economic Policy 2025): TFP up at most 0.66% over 10 years (about 0.07 points a year), and nearer 0.53% on his adjusted case [6]. Penn Wharton Budget Model, 8 Sep 2025: productivity and GDP level +1.5% by 2035, about +3% by 2055, +3.7% by 2075; growth contribution peaks at 0.2 points in 2032; inputs are 40% of labour income exposed and 25% labour-cost saving rising to 40% [7]. OECD (Filippucci, Gal, Schief, Nov 2024): TFP +0.25 to +0.6 points a year, labour productivity +0.4 to +0.9, over the next decade [8]. IMF Jan 2026 update: global growth +0.1 to +0.8 points a year medium term, up to +0.3 in 2026; a GDP figure, not productivity, seen via press only [9].
effect: Brynjolfsson, Li, Raymond: 5,172 customer-support agents at one Fortune 500 software firm; resolutions per hour +15% (QJE 2025; working paper said 14%), novices and low-skill about +34% (working paper) [10]. Dell'Acqua: 758 BCG consultants; inside the frontier +12.2% tasks done, about 25% faster, quality up more than 30% [5]. Noy and Zhang (Science 2023, 453 professionals, writing tasks): time -40%, quality +18%; RECALLED, not looked up.
grade: firm for adoption (about one firm in five); likely for task-level gains in support and writing; speculative for the macro size, since public ranges run from 0.07 to 0.6 points of productivity growth a year.

### 11. The four-day week
today: UK pilot 2022: 61 companies, about 2,900 staff, June-Dec 2022; over 90% kept it, 18 made it permanent (digest) [1]. Nature Human Behaviour 2025 (Fan, Schor, Kelly, Gu): 2,896 employees in 141 organisations across six countries, six-month trials, against a comparison of 285 staff in 12 firms; hours fell about 5 a week; burnout 2.83 to 2.38 (1-5 scale), job satisfaction 7.07 to 7.59 (0-10); gains held at 12 months except job satisfaction drifting back [2]. Iceland 2015-19: about 2,500 public-sector staff, 35-36 hours [3].
