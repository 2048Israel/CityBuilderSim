### 1. Hydrogen direct-reduced iron (HYBRIT, Stegra, SALCOS, thyssenkrupp)
today: Stegra (Boden, Sweden): EUR 1.4 bn round led by Wallenberg Investments agreed in principle Apr 2026, closed 24 Jun 2026; plant said to be over 60% built, all 37 alkaline electrolyser modules installed, 740 MW electrolysis, 100,000 t H2/yr [1][2]. HYBRIT: pilot research ended 2024; LKAB's full-scale 100%-H2 DRI plant at Gällivare is "being planned", 600 MW electrolyser, first step 1.2 Mt crude steel/yr, no start year or investment decision stated (page dated after 15 Jun 2026) [3][4]. Salzgitter SALCOS phase 1 (100 MW electrolyser, DRI, EAF) still due 2027 [5].
slips: Stegra first steel was end-2025, then 2026, now 2027, with the timeline "under review" in Jun 2026; a 100-day review found more capital needed and the CEO changed (Henriksson out, Håkan Buskhe in) [2]. Salzgitter pushed the investment decision for its stages 2-3 from 2026 to 2028-29 (Sep 2025) [5]. thyssenkrupp postponed its green-hydrogen tender for the 2.5 Mt/yr Duisburg DRI plant indefinitely (Mar 2025); ArcelorMittal cancelled EAF-DRI at Bremen and Eisenhüttenstadt (Jun 2025) [6].
projected: Stegra first steel 2027, 2.5 Mt/yr first phase, up to 5 Mt/yr by 2030 (company statements as reported by press, sources disagree on phase sizes) [2]. SALCOS phase 1 2027, later phases decision 2028-29 (Salzgitter CEO, Sep 2025) [5]. HYBRIT demonstration: no date found [3].
effect: Vogl, Åhman, Nilsson 2018: 3.48 MWh electricity per tonne of liquid steel (H2-DRI plus EAF, mostly electrolysis); cost range EUR 361-640 per t steel; competitive with the blast-furnace route at EUR 34-68 per t CO2 and EUR 40/MWh power [7]. kg H2 per t steel: not found in any page I opened (the commonly quoted ~50 kg/t was not confirmed). Derived: Stegra's 100,000 t H2/yr over 2.5 Mt/yr steel is about 40 kg H2 per t if all goes to reduction (pairing not confirmed). Coke/coal replaced per tonne: not found (the route removes the blast furnace, coke ovens and oxygen steel shop).
grade: firm for Stegra and SALCOS phase 1 (under construction, dated, but both slipping); likely for HYBRIT Gällivare; speculative for thyssenkrupp and ArcelorMittal.
