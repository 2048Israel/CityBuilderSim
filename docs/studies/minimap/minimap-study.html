A small map always in view, and a big city that does not turn into blobs

The city below is made up for the study: about a million people on 4.5 by 3 districts (35 by 23 km), a port, a core, rings of homes, industry along the port and the railway. It is drawn with the game's colours and each rule as the study describes it. It is not a save.

Why it blobs

Today a whole district is one colour

Far out, the map paints each 7.68 km district one flat tint: the kind of building that takes the most ground in it, stronger the fuller it is (MapTiles.tintOf()). A city of a dozen districts is a dozen squares, whatever the screen's size. The fix is to draw each district from where its buildings actually stand.

Three rules, three sizes

What each rule shows as the map gets smaller

Each picture at its real size in pixels. The stamp is what sits in the rail; the panel is the hover preview; the full width is the expanded map at the same zoom.
