Mockup 2 — A discovery arriving, in the inbox

Discoveries arrive as inbox notices (0.7.0's inbox: an envelope with a count; a notice unfolds in place with one button that goes where it is dealt with; resolved ones grey out and drop after 24 months). Two kinds: found here (a blue edge: "Researchers at … came up with …") and from abroad (a grey edge). Each says what it unlocks, who it affects with real figures, what it costs or whether a licence fee applies, and what the city's researchers do next. The screen behind is Policy › Research › Overview, dimmed.

A young city, June 1955 · the polio vaccine, from abroad (raised April 1955)
A big city, June 1979 · the wind farm, found by the Institute of technology this month
  • The text is written when the notice is raised and refreshed while it is live, as every notice's is (`Notice`); a discovery notice resolves once the player has opened its button's screen, or after 12 months (est.).
  • Polio figures: Canada's 1953 wave about 9,000 cases and 494 deaths; US paralytic cases fell about 90% within five years of the 1955 vaccine and 99.7% within ten (study §4, M3). The young city's four clinics and the outbreak share are illustrative.
  • The wind farm's output is first-generation (est.: today's 8,100 kW × 9% ÷ 40% capacity factor, study §4, E16); bigger turbines are a reserve discovery. Its D$ 31.0M is `buildings.json`'s sticker plus materials; the coal plant's D$ 5.1k a kW is the same arithmetic on its own figures. "94% of demand" is illustrative.
  • "About 1.5 years ahead" is history's date (December 1980) less the month found here; the model's big city found it in June 1979 (study §5.4).
