The game's one card for every building (0.7.25), with three additions: a NEW tag and a violet edge until the player has opened it; where it came from (found here, or from abroad and when); and its licence line (none for what the city found; a quarter of what it adds, until 20 years after history's date, for a patented one from abroad, study §2.4). The category chip with something new on it carries a violet dot, and a strip under the head says what is new.
