## Not sourced, or only partly

- Canada top 1% and top 10% wealth shares: StatCan's DHEA stops at quintiles and the SFS pages read gave no top-1% row. The US DFA stands in; Canada's 65.7% top-quintile share is the nearest.
- Capital gains tax rates across countries: not in the OECD database; the dividend rows stand in.
- Founder dilution: the only study found (Broughman and Fried, ECGI 2018: founder voting power 11.1% at IPO, 6.3% three years on, 18,000 US venture-backed firms) was seen as a search snippet, not read, and ECGI's terms were not checked. Evidence only.
- Median age of leaving home or forming a couple, and an annual inheritance flow for Canada: no StatCan or other open figure found.
- Cancellation date of the 2/3 inclusion (21 Mar 2025): press only. LCGE under s.110.6: not read.
- Mean first-birth age and the Canadian "about 26%" inheritance share are my arithmetic on published counts and shares.
- The Fed SCF 2025 (released 2026-10-09) supersedes the 2022 survey the brief named; both are given. The SCF 2025 report's text never mentions inheritance (searched), so the inheritance rows stay on SCF 2022.
