Mockup 4 — build cards: Offshore Platform, Alkylation Unit, Tanker Terminal

The game's one card for every building (0.7.25), row by row: head, tags, hero, price, bar 1 (money), bar 2 (the scarce resource), the investors' line, needs, the order bar, the quote, and the (i). Three groups are shown side by side, two from Industry and one from the new Ports page, in three states: the platform blocked by a gate, the alkylation unit with an investor's order on site, and the tanker terminal with the player's own order pending (pink border).

The big city of mockups 1–3, same month. Building figures are the research report's (§2.4, §3.2, §4.1), scaled to these sizes and marked (est.) there. "Ports" is a new Build category, drawn here as a city building (a blue square); question Q9 in the report asks Jerus whether ports should be the city's or a sector's.

  • Bar 2 is the scarce resource for each kind: sea for a platform (owned sea with an oil field, at most 150 m deep), land for a refinery unit, coast for a port. A platform's sea is about 1 km² with its 500 m safety zone.
  • The conversion unit's hero states its whole reason to exist in one line: what it turns into what, and the two prices that make the spread. That spread is the same figure as in mockup 2's units table and the investors' rule.
  • The port's bar 1 is "months of freight it saves", not "months of what it adds", because a port makes nothing. It earns by lowering the freight wedge on the city's seaborne trade (report §4.3 and Q9).
