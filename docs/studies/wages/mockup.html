Two pages: the minimum wage, a real minimum read three ways (against what unskilled work goes for, against the average wage, against what living alone costs), who it holds and what moving it does; and the ladder, one bar per qualification with its pay after cost.

Every figure below is measured: the playtest city of seed 0 at month 1,200, run with the floor a real minimum at 0.85 of the going rate (WageProbe, r085-s0; spec-wages.md, section 5). The nations on the average-wage gauge are the OECD's 33, as the game's own world file ships them (2025). Money is the game's, in today's dollars a month.

How to read it. If anyone could take any step, people would keep training until the steps paid about the same after cost: a step well above the one below says the seats, the fees or the wage needed to afford them are holding people back; a step at or under the one below says few will take it, and its holders will get scarce. Steps can invert, and the ladder draws them as they are.
