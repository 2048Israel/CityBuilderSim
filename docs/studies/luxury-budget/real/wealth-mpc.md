Range for a game dial: 1 to 6 cents per dollar of wealth per year across the studies read, with 3 to 3.5 cents the aggregate Federal Reserve staff figure and 3 to 5 cents the literature's rule of thumb. Housing runs higher than equity wealth in every study that splits them (Carroll et al. 9 vs 6; Fed note 5 vs 1; Case et al. qualitatively). Not found: a CBO or FRB/US coefficient read first-hand (two honest tries gave the FEDS note instead, which is the Fed staff's own).

Scale check against the Z.1 net worth in `networth.csv` (derived): 3.5 cents on $182.3 trillion (end of 2025) is about $6.4 trillion a year, 31% of 2025 PCE of $20.87 trillion. That is not a prediction; PCE is not caused by wealth alone, and the estimates are of the response to a change in wealth, not of the whole level. It only shows the order of magnitude the wealth channel could carry.
