What each chip does. Nations: a dot at each nation's largest place, its area by GDP, and its name with GDP and people written on its land where its ground in view is big enough to hold them (a small one keeps only its dot; hover it). Trade routes: this month's money each way between the city and each partner, violet what we sell, sand what we buy, the width by money; under D$0.3M a month none is drawn. Borders: Natural Earth's lines, dashed white; on at the far view and the globe, and at the near view only where a border crosses the city's ground. The three are remembered with the map's other settings.
The head and the legend. The two tiles are the window header's POPULATION and GDP, the same figures and sparklines, so the map, laid over the whole window, still says how big the city is. The legend folds to its head and stays folded until it is opened; it is no longer mouse-transparent, but only its head takes the click. The card opens on a click on a dot or a nation's land: its figures are the world file's for the city's year (WN1), its trade with us the city's own record (WN3), the ranks the 196 by the same figure; "All its measures" waits for the Rankings tab (spec-leaderboards).
