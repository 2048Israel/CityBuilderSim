Who buys your wood, and what the next cubic metre nets

Jerus: "if canda only needs 10 tons of wood, more wood would have to be sold esle where say portugal, thus transportation costs are higher". The page answers it for each good. For a Halifax city's lumber the first buyer is not Canada, which has wood to spare, but the United States, an hour's drive of freight away; it takes everything an ordinary city can saw. Canada and Portugal only start buying once the city sells about one cubic metre in twenty of the world's.

The curve is NationsModel.java's (beside this board): 196 nations, lumber's producers drawn like FAO's sawnwood table, its price scaled so that the city's floor with nothing sold is today's export price (US$250 a m³) at D$1.18 a US$. The world's use is FAO's 445 Mm³ of sawnwood (2023). The partners and their figures are invented for the board.

What moves it

Our own selling, this month−D$0.1 The world's answer, by January−D$0.4 The US building less (its year)−D$6.2 The freight to the USD$24.0 a m³

Each month our sales lower the price where they land, by that market's slope. Each January the world solves again with our year in it, and every nation's year: the city becomes the market only when it sells a real share of the world's lumber, as the curve shows.

The curve. Across, what the city sells a year as a share of the world's use, on a log scale; up, what the next m³ nets here. The violet line is the month (each buyer's own slope); the dashed one adds the world's answer that January's solve gives (spec-nations 4.4). The ticks are where each buyer starts to take any: the US at once, Cuba at 2.1%, Canada at 4.8%, Portugal at 5.6%. A city selling a twentieth of the world's lumber gets about 8% less a m³ than a small one, re-solved.
Where it comes from. The table is the city's trade by partner, kept from WN3 (today nothing records a partner: the trade is a sum by good). The haul is the freight table's: road and rail to the US and Canada through FR0's gateways, sea from the berth otherwise. Each partner's money is its own (WN4); the trade is settled in it, and the figures here are in D$ at this month's cross rate.
