Today the founding screen asks for the world's inflation and a seed for the ground, and the world beyond the city never moves. Now the world has a path, and the founder picks which: the real one from 2000, or one drawn from a seed. Click the two cards: the chart redraws.

Crude and the table are the World Bank's Pink Sheet (annual averages of the monthly file, over the US CPI-U, as ratios to 2025). The world beyond your city

196 nations trade with each other and with you. They grow, slump and recover; their prices are where they are and what they make. Pick the path they follow.

Its path

Historical

The real world from January 2000: each nation's growth, its people, its money and the price of what it makes, year by year to 2025. Then the UN's projected people and the game's own rule for output, with small shocks from the seed.

Random

The same world in January 2000, then a path of its own, drawn from the seed: booms, slumps, a crisis now and then, at the sizes the real world has had them.

Seed⚀ Rollused from 2026 on
The same seed gives the same world: two cities founded on it see the same nations do the same things, until your own trade moves their prices.
The dollar's own inflation

What the US dollar's prices average a year, as today's five chips. A Historical world replays what really moved – volumes, people, the prices of goods against each other, the moneys against the dollar – but never the dollar's own price level, so this choice holds in both.
On a generated world
A generated world has no Earth to stand on, so for the market the city stands at a real place on the coast drawn from its ground's seed – Bergen, Norway for seed 7,341 – and the nations are as far as they are from there. The map draws no borders; each partner is an arrow at the map's edge on its bearing.

What is saved. The path (Historical or Random) and its seed, nothing else: every nation's year is a pure function of those two and the year (spec-nations 7), so a save carries two fields and the world file. The January solve's answer is saved too, since your trade is in it (WN3).
What the harnesses do. A harness city founds a Random world on a fixed seed, as today's draws are fixed by the month; two runs of one city still come out identical (ForeignCheck), and a check of the market can found two seeds and assert they differ. LongPlaytest takes the path and the seed as properties.
