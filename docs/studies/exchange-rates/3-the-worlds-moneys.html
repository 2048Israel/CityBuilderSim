Each nation's money. Its prices against the US's (the trend); the dollar cycle (half-life five years); a risk-off about once in eight years (havens up, high yielders down); its terms of trade from the January solve; its own swing (3.5-year half-life). Pegs are held, and broken by the vault and a chance that rises with the gap. A Historical game takes the real monthly rates, 2000-2025.
What the prototype measured. Over 1,000 seeded 26-year paths: euro 8.9% a year, yen 9.9%, pound 8.6%, franc 9.1%, Canadian dollar 8.1%, Australian dollar 12.3%, an emerging float 16.6% (1999-2025's real ones: 9.3%, 9.4%, 8.5%, 9.6%, 8.4%, 11.8%, 11-18%). Half-lives, measured as an economist would, 3.5-4.9 years. The dollar's widest swing in 26 years: 38% (median). The carry trade, long A$ short yen: 4.0% a year, Sharpe 0.27, monthly skew −1.34, worst month −34%. A month of all 196 moneys and the D$ costs about 10 microseconds.
