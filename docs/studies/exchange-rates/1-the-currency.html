The rule. The rate is parity's trend, times the fundamentals' level, times the market. Parity's trend is followed one for one, with no lag. The fundamentals are the real rate gap times how long the market expects it (2.5 years, capped at 35%), the balance, and the vault's cover. The market is its own swing: seeded, fading with a 3.5-year half-life, about 2.2% a month.
What changed from today. Today a pull of 0.4% a month toward a moving parity left the D$ 24-35% from it for decades. Parity counted rent (41-80% of the basket). The rate moved 1.2% a year and one way for up to 78 months. Under the proposal his city's D$ moves about 9.5% a year and never more than about 14 months one way.
