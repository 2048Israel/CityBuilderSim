The central bank, decided in steps and kept as the Fed keeps its books

Five boards for docs/studies/spec-central-bank.md. Every figure is from Jerus's own cities, read from the saves without writing them, or from copies of them played on in a scratch folder in 0.8.11. The rate moves in quarter points at scheduled decisions, a range a quarter point wide with an overnight rate trading inside it, and a central bank whose remittance is its income statement.

The remittance puzzle

With no paper held and cash in the till, the remittance is the window's interest: the commercial bank borrowing past its deposits at the dial plus 0.25. In Halifax it reached 14% of revenue. The 37 months over a quarter, up to 44%, were the treasury's own overdraft interest coming back to it. Neither shows on any screen.

The dial today

The autopilot sets the dial every month to the rule on year-on-year inflation. In a copy of Halifax played 20 years on it moved 230 times and took 227 different values, up to 3.26 points in a month.

The proposal

A range like 3.50-3.75%, decided every two months in quarter points (at most ¾ at a decision, up to a point unscheduled when the rule is 3 points away). The overnight rate trades between reserves' rate and the window. The remittance is the bank's own income statement.

Decisions

1A range, not a number. Moved only in quarter points. The rule's advice is the rate it wants the overnight at, and the range chosen is the one that puts the overnight nearest it.

2Decisions on a calendar. Every two months by default; Jerus's every three is the other setting. At a decision the autopilot moves toward the rule by at most ¾ of a point, and holds if the rule is within an eighth.

3Unscheduled, rarely. If the rule runs 3 points from the overnight between decisions, the autopilot decides at once, by up to a point. In the copy: 3 times in 20 years. The player's "Decide now" is the same thing, by hand.

4The player's hand waits. A range set by hand takes effect at the next decision, unless they decide now. Either way the decision log records it.

Board 2 · Policy › Central bank, the corridor · CB2

The overnight rate inside the range

Three rates the central bank sets, and one the market makes between them: the reverse repo at the bottom, interest on reserves ten points under the top, the window at the top. The overnight sits at reserves' rate while the bank holds spare cash and climbs to the window as it borrows there.

The central bank's books, as the Fed keeps them

Board 5 · Finances › Your rate · CD1, amended

What the market expects, in steps

CD1 prices the curve on the dial the market expects: a smooth drift to the rule. With decisions in steps, the expected path is flat until the next decision and moves at each, by at most ¾ of a point. The city behind the curve: 5% inflation, the dial held at 1% by hand, credibility 60%.
